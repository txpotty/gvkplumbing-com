    Older homes often have narrow pits that will not fit a submersible. A pedestal pump fits where nothing else will, and we install it with rigid discharge, a quality check valve, and a float rod set to your pit depth so it starts before water reaches the top of the liner. Noisy, but they last.

    Residential Basement Sump Pump Installation in Chaparral

    A typical house pit holds an 18 by 22 inch basin with a 1/3 horsepower pump, and that covers most homes.     Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Chaparral

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Chaparral.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Chaparral

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We test it under load.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Chaparral, NM

    Submersible units sit down in the pit, sealed, with the water around them cooling the motor. That makes them quieter than a pedestal, and in Chaparral, NM we set them on a couple of bricks so the intake stays above the silt that settles on the bottom of every liner. Sealed wiring matters here.
