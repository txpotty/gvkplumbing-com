    Pedestal Sump Pumps in Chaparral

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They typically last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod set to your pit depth.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, basement sump pump installation involves real plumbing, electrical work and proper discharge routing.     Usually, standard home insurance policies do not cover a new sump pump installation or repairs unless the damage was caused by a sudden, accidental event like a burst pipe, rather than gradual wear and tear or groundwater seepage.     A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, built to handle everyday water removal.     Swapping a pump in an existing pit typically takes two to three hours. A new pit with slab cutting and concrete patching usually takes most of a day.
