    Curbless Shower Installation in Chaparral

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. We plan the slope with your contractor before demo.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Chaparral

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Chaparral, NM

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. Valves, supplies and drains go in at framing, with valve depth matched to the finish and drains set for your pan. Then the rough-in inspection. We return for trim after tile.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Chaparral

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. The slope is our job. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Digital Shower Systems in Chaparral, NM

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. These valves usually sit in a nearby closet or vanity and need low-voltage wiring to the controller and an outlet close by. An electrician handles the outlet. We plumb the valve and outlets.
