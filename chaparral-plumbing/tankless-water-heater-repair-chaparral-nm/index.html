    Condensing Tankless Repair in Chaparral, NM

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. That condensate runs through a trap and, where code or the manual calls for it, a neutralizer filled with limestone or marble chips before it reaches the drain. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Tankless Flush and Scale Removal in Chaparral

    Lukewarm water at high flow and a unit that runs loud are two early signs of scale.     Navien Tankless Water Heater Repair in Chaparral

    Navien condensing units run a second stainless exchanger that pulls heat from the exhaust. We clear the trap, check the PVC vent pitch and test the circulation pump on NPE-A models with built-in recirculation. Then we fire it at low and high flow.
