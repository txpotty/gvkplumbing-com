    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer in Deming

    Annual residential testing runs $75 to $150 per assembly. A rebuild usually costs $150 to $450. New residential installs range from $300 to $1,200, and commercial RPZ assemblies often cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed once the drain and enclosure are included.

    Installation and repair should be left to a licensed plumber who holds tester certification. Installing the wrong device, mounting a PVB too low or burying an RPZ in a pit can each fail inspection and leave the water unprotected. Providers can fine for it.

    An RPZ has two checks with a relief valve between them, and it is rated for high-hazard lines. A DCVA has two checks and no relief valve, so it is used for lower hazards like many fire lines and some irrigation systems. Code decides which one.

    A brass assembly often lasts 10 to 20 years, sometimes longer. The rubber parts inside wear out much sooner, which is why rebuild kits exist and why most failed tests are fixed without replacing the body. Freezes shorten that life.
