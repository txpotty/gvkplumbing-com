    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Deming, NM

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Emergency Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Deming

    An ejector failure often starts with the high water alarm, followed by slow drains and a smell near the pit. Stop all use of the basement fixtures and call, because once a plumber is on site most emergency replacements take just a few hours. Trucks stock popular models.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Deming

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Basement Bathroom Ejector Systems in Deming

    Basement bathrooms sit below the building sewer, so the toilet, shower and sink drain to a sealed basin instead of flowing by gravity. We saw the floor, trench drains to the basin, set it, carry a two-inch vent to the roof and run discharge to the main drain through a check valve and shutoff. Then we patch the concrete.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Deming, NM

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Deming

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in Deming, NM

A new system with a basin, slab excavation, venting through the roof, electrical work and an alarm costs more, but it is the only way to add a bathroom below the sewer line in most homes without a macerating unit. Ask for both numbers.
