    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Deming

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections.     Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Deming, NM

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Deming

    Water softeners combat hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance prolongs its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing costly scale damage.
