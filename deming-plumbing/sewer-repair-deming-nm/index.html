    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Deming

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Deming, NM

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Deming, NM

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Waste collects in the dip. Expansive clay soils in some areas swell and shrink with moisture, which can crack slab foundations and the pipes that run beneath them. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and reset the slope to the quarter inch per foot most codes call for on residential laterals. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.
