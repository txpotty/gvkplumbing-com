    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Deming, NM

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Deming

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Deming

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Deming

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Deming, NM

    Bought the toilet yourself? Check the rough-in printed on the box first. Before the old toilet comes out, we inspect the new one for shipping cracks, because cracked china goes back to the store, not onto your flange. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Deming

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. We follow the maker's frame height chart exactly. A frame set wrong puts the bowl too low or too high, and once tile is up there is no adjusting it. Measure twice here.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Deming

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Tightening the tank bolts evenly, side to side, matters more than how hard they go, because an uneven pull cracks the china or leaves a slow drip at one bolt. It should not wobble.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Deming

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. It is set that way so a grab bar fits. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Deming, NM

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. Cleaning gets easier, and the bowl height can be set anywhere within the carrier range, useful for a tall family. Service happens through the flush plate.
