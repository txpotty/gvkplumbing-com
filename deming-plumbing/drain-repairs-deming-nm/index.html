    Clogged Drain Repair in Deming

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning.     Recurring Clog Repair in Deming

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Cleaning will not stop it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Deming, NM

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Deming, NM

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, and we replace the bad length with plastic pipe joined to the old iron with shielded couplings. If the whole stack is failing, we price it separately.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Deming, NM

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. They hide well. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Every repair is done to code.
