    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Gallup

    The cost for sewage ejector pump installation in Gallup usually ranges from $2,500 to $5,000.     Signs that you might need a new sewage ejector pump include unusual noises from the pump or pit, frequent cycling on and off, slow drainage from basement fixtures or a sewage odor coming from the area. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional check your system to prevent a complete failure and costly backups.

    Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves occasionally checking the basin for debris, confirming the check valve works correctly and testing the float switch. It is also wise to avoid flushing non-biodegradable items. GVK Plumbing offers maintenance services to keep your pump running efficiently and prevent surprise breakdowns.

    New ejector installations usually require a plumbing permit and inspection. Straight pump replacements may not, and we confirm local requirements for every job.

    We primarily install high-quality, reputable brands such as Liberty Pumps and Zoeller. These manufacturers are known for their durability, reliability and efficient performance, providing a long-lasting solution for your home. We can discuss the best brand and model for your needs.

    A standard sewage ejector pump installation typically takes one to two days. More complex installations, like those requiring significant trenching or electrical panel upgrades, may take longer.

    A sewage ejector pump is designed to handle solid waste and wastewater from plumbing fixtures below the main sewer line, like toilets and sinks, discharging it into the sewer system. A sump pump, in contrast, removes groundwater from a basement or crawl space, only handling clear water to prevent flooding. They serve different purposes for different types of water.
