    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Gallup, NM

    With PEX-A, the tech slips an expansion ring onto the pipe and stretches both with a battery tool.     Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Gallup

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. The test happens before drywall goes up.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Gallup, NM

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A bends the easiest and uses expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and uses crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C handles more like B. All three are code-listed for drinking water. We match the pipe to the fittings and tools from one maker so the warranty stays intact.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Gallup

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. That takes seconds. Skipping it is how leaks start.
