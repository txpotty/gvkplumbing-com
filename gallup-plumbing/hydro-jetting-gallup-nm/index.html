    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Gallup

    It is safe for most pipes in fair condition when pressure is set correctly. We camera the line first and avoid jetting pipe that is broken or caved in.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is far more powerful and requires training to operate safely and effectively.
