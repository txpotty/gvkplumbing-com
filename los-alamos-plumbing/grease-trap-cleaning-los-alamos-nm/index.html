    Grease Line Jetting in Los Alamos

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. It builds up in rings, thickest at the bottom of the pipe. Snaking just bores a small path that closes up in weeks, but a rotating jetter nozzle strips grease off the full wall of the pipe. We run a camera after to confirm.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Los Alamos, NM

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. Busy kitchens need them cleaned every week or two.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in Los Alamos, NM

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. Our jetters run around 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle, which scours the whole inside of the pipe and pushes the loosened grease downstream. Afterward a camera pass confirms the pipe is clean.
