    Softener Loop Installation in Los Alamos

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service easy.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Los Alamos

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. GVK Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Los Alamos home.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Los Alamos

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Los Alamos home. This means every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including needed bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide complete protection against hard water throughout your property.
