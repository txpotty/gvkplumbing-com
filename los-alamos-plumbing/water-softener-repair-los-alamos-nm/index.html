    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Los Alamos, NM

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. GVK Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Los Alamos

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Los Alamos, NM

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. The valve gets tested in place.
