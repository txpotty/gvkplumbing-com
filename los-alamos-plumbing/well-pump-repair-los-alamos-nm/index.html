    Pressure Switch Replacement in Los Alamos

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing.     Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. Breaker trips usually come first. With the power locked out, we test and replace the failed parts and tighten every terminal. Wiring meets New Mexico code when the job is done.
