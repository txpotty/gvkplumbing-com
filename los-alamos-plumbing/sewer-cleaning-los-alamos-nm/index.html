    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Los Alamos, NM

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Los Alamos

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. A jetter sends high-pressure water around the full circumference of the pipe, removing buildup that a cable would leave behind.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Los Alamos

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Los Alamos

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. A sewer joint is a steady source. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out, then follow with a jetter if fine root hairs are left behind, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    After-Hours Sewer Backups in Los Alamos

    Backups happen at bad times. Nights and weekends included. The after-hours rate is quoted on the phone before we dispatch, and the tech arrives ready to clear the main, set up a wet vac and photograph the damage for your insurance company.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Los Alamos, NM

    Grease and scale are jetting work. Cables leave them behind. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Los Alamos

    Roots come back after cutting. Plan on regrowth in 12 to 18 months. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Los Alamos, NM

    Grease hardens as it cools. Then it narrows the pipe. Kitchen grease coats the sewer line the same way plaque coats an artery, and over a few years the opening can close to the size of a finger, which is when drains start to back up, and jetting is the right fix.
