    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Los Alamos

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Los Alamos homes with cast iron or clay pipes.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Los Alamos

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup snags debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and ends corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Los Alamos, NM

    A cleanout provides easy access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on future maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Los Alamos, NM

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging. Methods like pipe bursting or pipe lining allow us to replace or repair your sewer line with minimal excavation, often only requiring two access points. This can save your lawn, driveway, and landscaping from extensive damage, making it a common choice for homeowners in Los Alamos who want to preserve their property's appearance. We'll determine if your sewer line is a good candidate for this advanced technique.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Los Alamos, NM

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, at the same time breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are ideal for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Los Alamos.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Los Alamos

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.
