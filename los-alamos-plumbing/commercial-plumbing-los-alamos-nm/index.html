    Grease Interceptor and Line Service in Los Alamos, NM

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Los Alamos, NM

    Gyms, commercial kitchens and apartment buildings burn through hot water in short, heavy peaks. We install and service high-input tank heaters, tankless racks and boiler-fed indirect systems with storage tanks. Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. Mineral scale chokes output quickly. We put the heaters on a descaling schedule and install mixing valves so hand-wash sinks get tempered water at a safe temperature.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Los Alamos

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Los Alamos

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. The camera goes in at a cleanout, we note distances to every defect and then locate each one from the surface with a receiver. You get the video file. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Los Alamos, NM

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. When pinholes keep moving around the building, repiping the worst zones in Type L copper or PEX usually beats paying for more emergency visits. We map the system first. Then we phase the work by floor or by tenant so the building stays open.
