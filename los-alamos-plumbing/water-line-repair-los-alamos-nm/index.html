    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Los Alamos

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Los Alamos

    Trenchless work makes sense when the line runs under a driveway, patio or a row of mature shrubs. We dig a pit at the meter and another at the house. The new line travels between them without a trench. A pneumatic boring tool or a cable puller drags HDPE or PEX through the soil, or through the old pipe if it is a straight run, usually in one working day from start to backfill.

    Commercial Water Line Repair in Los Alamos, NM

    Commercial service lines are often 1 1/2 to 4 inch pipe feeding a meter bank, a backflow assembly and sometimes a fire line. A break shuts the business down. GVK Plumbing can schedule repairs early in the morning or after closing, and we bring the larger fittings and couplings on the truck. We also arrange the backflow test and inspection that most water providers require after work on the commercial side of the meter.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Los Alamos

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. First step is a pressure gauge on the outside spigot. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Galvanized pipe can lose half its opening to rust scale. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Underground Water Line Leak Repair in Los Alamos

    Underground leaks hide well. Many people find out from the bill before they see anything in the yard.     Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Los Alamos, NM

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Los Alamos

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Los Alamos, NM

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.
