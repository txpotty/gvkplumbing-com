    Perma-Liner and NuFlow Lining Systems in Los Alamos

    Perma-Liner and NuFlow both make lining systems we use, each with its own resin chemistry and installation equipment. Both are built to ASTM standards for cured-in-place pipe. The brand matters less than the prep, because a liner bonded to a dirty, scaly pipe wall will not hold the way the manufacturer rates it. We clean to bare pipe first.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Los Alamos, NM

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repairs in Los Alamos

    Sewage coming up through a floor drain is an emergency, but the first fix is usually a cable or a jetter to get the line flowing again. The permanent trenchless repair follows once the pipe is open and on camera. GVK Plumbing can often schedule the liner within a few days. In the meantime the house drains normally.

    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Los Alamos, NM

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years.
