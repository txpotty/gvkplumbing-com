    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Los Alamos, NM

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Expansive clay soils in some areas swell and shrink with moisture, which can shift slab foundations and the pipes that run beneath them. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. Both options get a price.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Los Alamos, NM

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. Spot fixes suit healthy systems.     Pinhole Leak Repair in Los Alamos

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. One usually means more are coming.
