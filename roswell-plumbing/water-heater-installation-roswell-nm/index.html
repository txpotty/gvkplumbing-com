    Gas Water Heater Installation in Roswell, NM

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Roswell, NM

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. Before hooking up the new tank we check that the breaker and wire size match the elements, since a mismatch is a common cause of tripped breakers and scorched terminals. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Roswell

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Roswell

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien each spell out clearances, vent lengths and relief valve placement, and a warranty claim can be denied if those were ignored. Our crews install to spec. That protects your coverage.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Roswell, NM

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Roswell

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Roswell

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Venting is what keeps CO out. All work follows local code.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Roswell, NM

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. Shut the cold supply valve on top of the tank and turn the gas knob to pilot or flip the breaker, then call us. Common tanks ride on our trucks. Most emergency swaps finish the same day.
