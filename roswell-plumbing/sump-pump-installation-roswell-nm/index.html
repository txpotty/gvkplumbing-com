    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Roswell, NM

    Severe storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and charging units. Many include alarms that warn you about problems. Backups add an extra layer of protection for finished basements.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Roswell, NM

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is a concern. Our team is experienced in installing these pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Roswell, NM.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Roswell

    A high water alarm alerts you when water rises above normal levels. Smart monitors can send alerts to your phone when the pump runs or fails. We install alarms and Wi-Fi monitors on new and existing pumps. These devices are particularly useful for vacation homes and rental properties. Early warnings can prevent major water damage.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Roswell

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, running quietly and taking up less space. They are usually more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, matched to the particular needs of your Roswell property.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Roswell

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Roswell

    Some homes collect water in sunken patios, below-grade driveways or window wells rather than a basement. We install exterior sump basins with pumps that move storm water to a safe discharge point. These systems are useful for lots that slope toward the house. We route discharge lines so water drains away from the foundation and neighbors. This helps prevent siding damage and foundation problems.
