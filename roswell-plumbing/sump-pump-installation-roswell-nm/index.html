    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Roswell

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go.     New Sump Pit Installation in Roswell

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Roswell, NM

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Roswell

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Roswell, NM

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. They make matching backup units. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Roswell

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Roswell, NM

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. Our trucks carry 1/3 and 1/2 HP submersibles, check valves and fittings, so in most homes we can pull the old pump and set a new one in one visit. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Roswell, NM

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.
