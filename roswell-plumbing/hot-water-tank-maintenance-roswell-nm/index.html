    Sediment Flushing in Roswell, NM

    Sediment is mostly calcium carbonate and sand, and it settles in a layer that can reach several inches on a neglected 50-gallon tank. The heat has to push through it to reach the water. The drain hose runs to a floor drain or outside, never onto a lawn where hot water can burn the grass.
