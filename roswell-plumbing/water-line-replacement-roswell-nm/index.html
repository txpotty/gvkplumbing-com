    Water Line Materials We Install in Roswell, NM

    Our trucks carry Type K copper, PEX-a and HDPE. Each one fits a different job. Copper suits short runs and high pressure, PEX handles long runs with bends, and HDPE is the usual pick for trenchless pulls and bores, where we need a tough, fused pipe that can be dragged a long way. All of it is listed for drinking water, meeting NSF 61.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Roswell

    Galvanized steel was common in homes built before the 1960s. Inside, the pipe rusts once the zinc is gone. After a few decades the opening can be half its original size, and that shows up as weak pressure and orange water, and no amount of cleaning brings it back. It has to come out.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Roswell, NM

    Polybutylene is the gray or blue plastic pipe installed from roughly the late 1970s to the mid 1990s. Chlorine in the water breaks it down from the inside. The failures tend to be sudden splits rather than slow drips, and some insurers will not write a policy on a house that still has it. Worth replacing.

    Commercial Water Line Replacement in Roswell

    Larger buildings bring larger pipe, deeper trenches and more inspection steps. Backflow testing and the report to the water provider are part of the job. Trench shoring comes into play below five feet, and we plan the work so the building is without water for hours, not days, often running a temporary supply while the new line is tied in. Night work is possible.

    Trenchless Bores Under Driveways in Roswell

    Directional boring drills a path under the obstacle and pulls the pipe back through it. It does not need the old pipe as a guide. We use it when the new line has to take a different route, say around a new addition or under a driveway poured after the house was built, and the launch and exit pits stay small. Heavy rock is the exception.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Roswell, NM

    Pipe pulling works like this. Two pits get dug, one at each end. A cable or bursting head goes through the old pipe and drags new HDPE or PEX back through the same path, splitting the old pipe if it is brittle, so a driveway, a row of shrubs or a concrete walk can stay where it is.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Roswell

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, connect it with brass or poly fittings that are approved underground, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Roswell, NM

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.
