    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Roswell

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. GVK Plumbing expertly installs many models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Roswell home.

    Softener Loop Installation in Roswell

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Roswell

    Most water softener installations cost $1,500 to $4,000 including equipment. Adding a new softener loop usually adds $500 to $1,500 depending on access.

    Common signs of hard water include soap scum buildup on fixtures and shower doors, dry skin and hair, dull laundry and scale buildup inside pipes and appliances like water heaters. We can perform a water hardness test in your Roswell home to confirm your need for a softener.

    Salt consumption varies widely depending on the water hardness, your household's water usage and the efficiency of the softener system. On average, a family of four in a hard water area might use 40 to 80 pounds of salt per month. High-efficiency models usually use less salt.

    Most standard water softener installations can be completed within 3 to 6 hours. More complex installations, or those requiring major plumbing modifications, might take a full day. Our plumbers work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home's water supply.
