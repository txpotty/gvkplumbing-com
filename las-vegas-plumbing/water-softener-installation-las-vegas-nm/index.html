    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Las Vegas

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps performing.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Las Vegas

    The cost of water softener installation in Las Vegas, NM typically ranges from $1,500 to $4,000, depending on the system type, brand and complexity of the installation. Factors like existing plumbing modifications and the size of your home can affect the final price. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after an on-site assessment.

    With proper maintenance, a quality water softener system can last between 10 and 15 years, and sometimes even longer. The lifespan depends on the quality of the unit, the hardness of your water and how well it is maintained. Regular servicing can help lengthen its life.

    We offer a warranty on our workmanship, and the water softener units themselves come with manufacturer warranties, which typically range from 5 to 10 years for the tank and control valve. We will provide you with all warranty details for your particular system after installation.

    White scale on faucets, spotty dishes, dry skin and reduced water heater performance are common signs. A water test verifies hardness levels.

    Many homes benefit from a softener when the water is hard. Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. Softeners protect water heaters, fixtures and appliances from scale.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. Many homeowners add an RO system at the kitchen sink for drinking and cooking.

    Installing on an existing loop typically takes two to three hours. Adding a new loop can take half a day or more.
