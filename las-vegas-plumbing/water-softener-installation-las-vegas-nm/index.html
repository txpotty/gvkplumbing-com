    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Las Vegas

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Las Vegas, NM

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. GVK Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. They work best on very hard water.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Las Vegas

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. We install several models.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Las Vegas

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet, and many can also feed a refrigerator water line or an ice maker. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Las Vegas, NM

    Building a home in Las Vegas, NM? New construction is the cheapest time to add a softener loop, because the walls are open and the main can be routed past the garage or utility room without cutting finished drywall. We work with builders. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Las Vegas

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They work best on moderate water.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Las Vegas, NM

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Newer valves meter actual water use, some connect to Wi-Fi for salt alerts and higher-capacity tanks regenerate less often for larger households. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Las Vegas, NM
