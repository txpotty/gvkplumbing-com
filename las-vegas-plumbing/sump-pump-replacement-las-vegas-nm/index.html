    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Las Vegas

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats work best in larger pits.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Las Vegas

    The cost of a sump pump replacement in Las Vegas, NM can range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump, its horsepower and the complexity of the installation. Factors like needing a new sump pit, major re-piping or adding a battery backup system can influence the final price. We provide transparent, upfront quotes after assessing your needs.

    If storms knock out power in your area or you have finished living space below grade, a backup is a wise investment. A battery unit with a high-water alarm usually runs six to ten hours of intermittent pumping on a full charge.

    We install a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you pick the best brand and model for your home's requirements and budget.

    In many areas, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements usually do not require a permit. However, if the replacement involves major changes to the electrical system, discharge piping or the sump pit itself, a permit might be necessary. Our team will advise you on local code requirements in Las Vegas.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be finished within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    It is a good idea to test your sump pump at least every three months, and especially before and after rainy seasons. To test, simply pour a bucket of water into the sump pit. The pump should activate, drain the water and then shut off automatically. If it does not, it is time for a professional inspection.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to turn on automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is critical for maintaining flood protection during power outages caused by severe weather.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and make sure the discharge pipe is clear. An annual professional inspection by GVK Plumbing can also help lengthen its lifespan.
