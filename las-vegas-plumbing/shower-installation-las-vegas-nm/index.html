    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Las Vegas, NM

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. No high curbs. ADA layouts are available on request.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Las Vegas, NM

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves step up from there, holding an exact temperature and letting you set volume separately, which suits rain heads, handhelds and body sprays. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Emergency Shower Replacement in Las Vegas

    A cracked pan or a failed valve can force a replacement fast. When water is getting under the floor or into a ceiling below, we pull the damaged unit, dry what we can and install a standard stall so the bathroom is usable again within a day or two. Custom work can wait. We check the framing before the new unit goes in.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Las Vegas

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Las Vegas

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. A gauge reading comes first.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Las Vegas

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Las Vegas

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Shower Stall Installation in Las Vegas

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. Then the drain goes in. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.
