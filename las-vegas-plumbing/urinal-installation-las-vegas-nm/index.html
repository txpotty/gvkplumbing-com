    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Las Vegas

    The cost for urinal installation in Las Vegas, NM can range from $400 to $1,200, depending on the type of urinal, complexity of plumbing modifications, and whether it's a new installation or a replacement. This price typically includes labor and basic materials, but highly specialized units or extensive rough-in work can increase the overall cost.

    A replacement takes two to four hours. A new install with rough-in and carrier usually takes one to two days, plus inspection time.

    GVK Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on all urinal installations. This covers our labor and craftsmanship for a specified period, giving you peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be provided with your service agreement.

    While it might seem like a way to save money, urinal installation involves precise plumbing connections, proper venting, and secure mounting that are best handled by a professional. Incorrect installation can lead to leaks, odors, and expensive repairs down the line, making professional service a worthwhile investment.

    A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more forcefully down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.

    Signs that your existing urinal might need replacement include frequent clogs, persistent odors despite cleaning, visible cracks or extensive staining, or if it's an older, inefficient model that uses excessive water. Our technicians can assess its condition and advise on repair versus replacement.

    Flush urinals use a water flush to remove waste, while waterless urinals use a specialized trap system with a sealant liquid to prevent odors without water. Waterless urinals offer major water savings but require specific maintenance for their sealant cartridges. Both types offer effective waste disposal.

    Switching from a 1 gpf urinal to a 0.125 gpf model saves most of the water per flush. In a busy restroom that adds up to many thousands of gallons a year.
