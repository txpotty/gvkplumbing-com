    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Las Vegas, NM

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Las Vegas

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. First we stop the leak and get water back on with a temporary fix, then we schedule the repipe without pressure. That gives you time. Insurance may cover the water damage from the burst even if it does not cover the repipe itself, so we document what we find with photos.

    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Las Vegas, NM

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Las Vegas, NM

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. We make the house safe and usable first, with temporary repairs that get you water, then schedule the full repipe as soon as the crew and permit allow. We move fast. Most emergency repipes still finish within the normal two to four day window once work starts.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Las Vegas, NM

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 once access and restoration are included. The numbers add up quickly. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself, and we give you a side-by-side comparison so you can make that call with real numbers in front of you.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Las Vegas

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Las Vegas

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other. We can usually identify it from the pipe at the water heater, and full replacement makes the house easier to insure and sell.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Las Vegas, NM

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. Our crew cuts neat rectangles, covers nearby surfaces and patches each opening after the pressure test. Textures get matched. Knockdown, orange peel and smooth finishes are all routine, and your walls are ready for primer and paint when we leave.
