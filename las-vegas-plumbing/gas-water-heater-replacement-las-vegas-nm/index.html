    Picking a Gas Water Heater for Your Las Vegas, NM Home

    No Hot Water Calls in Las Vegas

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Las Vegas

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Commercial Gas Water Heater Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Commercial gas heaters in restaurants and offices often run 75 to 100 gallons at 75,000 BTU or more, and they need a gas line sized for that load. Code is stricter here. We size by peak demand and recovery, not tank volume alone.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Las Vegas

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most New Mexico homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We measure the closet before ordering. Most of these swaps take two to three hours.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Water on the floor around the tank usually means the inner tank has rusted through, and that is not a repair. Close the cold water valve above the heater and set the gas knob to off. After that, give us a call. Common sizes are on the truck, so same-day hot water is normal.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Las Vegas, NM

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. We set them up per the factory manual and test draft.

    Bradford White Tank Replacement in Las Vegas

    Most of the tanks on our trucks are Bradford White, partly because their Defender combustion system and ICON gas valve are simple to service. We help you match model to demand. Then it gets installed to code.
