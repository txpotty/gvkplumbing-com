    Pipe Repair and Repiping for Older Homes in Las Vegas, NM

    Galvanized steel and polybutylene are the two pipes we replace most. Homes built in the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail and are worth replacing. Repipes are done in PEX or Type L copper, running new lines through the attic and walls with small access holes, and the water stays on at night for most of the job. Expect two to four days.

    Emergency Home Plumbing in Las Vegas, NM

    Burst pipes do not keep business hours. We take emergency calls for burst lines, overflowing toilets and total loss of water across Las Vegas, NM, and the dispatcher talks you through closing the main shutoff while the truck is on the way. The first visit usually stops the water and fixes the cause.

    Pressure Regulators and Shutoff Valves in Las Vegas, NM

    Water pressure over 80 psi wears out hoses and valves early. We test static pressure with a gauge on a hose bib, and if it reads high we install or replace a pressure reducing valve and set it in the 50 to 70 psi range most fixtures are rated for. Seized main shutoffs get replaced too.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Las Vegas

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. The truck has cartridges for the big brands, Moen and Delta included, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old stops fail when touched.

    Water Heater Repair and Installation in Las Vegas

    A water heater works hardest in winter, when incoming water is coldest. We repair and install tank, tankless, gas and electric units from Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai, and the usual culprits are a heavy layer of sediment or a thermocouple that has quit reading the pilot. No hot water is often a quick part swap. New units get an expansion tank and pan when the code requires it.

    Toilet Repair and Replacement in Las Vegas

    A running toilet can waste a couple hundred gallons a day. Most fixes are a flapper, a fill valve or a new wax ring, and when the bowl is cracked or the toilet is older than the last bathroom remodel, we set a new water-saving model and level it on the flange. A toilet that rocks gets shimmed so the wax seal holds.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Las Vegas

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. Our plumbers swap failing pumps for cast iron submersibles and replace stuck floats and check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. Pour a bucket in to test it.

    Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in Las Vegas

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning. It gets replaced with PEX or copper, same as rusted galvanized and pinholed copper, routing new lines through the attic and walls so the slab stays intact and the house keeps water each night. Drywall patches are included.
