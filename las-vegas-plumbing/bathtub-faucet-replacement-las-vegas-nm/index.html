    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Las Vegas

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We replace Moen and Delta cartridges or old compression stems and seats, and we grease the O-rings with silicone plumber's grease so the handle turns smoothly for years. Seat wrenches come out often. About an hour, usually.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Las Vegas

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. We find galvanized supplies that rust shut, valves soldered to copper with no stops anywhere, and spouts threaded onto steel nipples that snap when you try to turn them. Adapters for old pipe ride along. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Las Vegas, NM

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A cartridge plus a same-brand trim kit can refresh an old faucet for far less than a new valve. That option comes up first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Las Vegas

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
