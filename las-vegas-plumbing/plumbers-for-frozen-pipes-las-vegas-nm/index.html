    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Las Vegas

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line somewhere. Copper and galvanized pipes can be thawed with an electric pipe thawing machine that warms the metal from the inside. PEX and CPVC are warmed slowly with heat guns or infrared heaters to avoid softening the plastic.
