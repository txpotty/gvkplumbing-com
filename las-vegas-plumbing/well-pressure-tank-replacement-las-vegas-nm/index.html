    What Goes Into a Well Tank Swap in Las Vegas, NM

    Flex-Lite Pressure Tank Replacement in Las Vegas

    Flex-Lite tanks have a composite shell. Rust is not a concern. That makes them a good pick for damp basements, crawlspaces and well houses where steel tanks tend to corrode from the bottom up, and they are lighter to carry into tight spaces. Same plumbing as steel.

    Cost-Effective Well Pressure Tank Upgrades in Las Vegas, NM

    Upgrading a size often costs less than people think. The difference between two tank sizes is usually modest. A larger tank means fewer pump starts per day, and fewer starts is the biggest single thing you can do for pump life, so we often recommend it during a replacement.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, with short cycling, pressure swings and water spitting from the air valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Las Vegas

    The tank tee is the brass manifold that connects tank, switch, gauge, drain and relief valve. Old ones leak at threads. The relief valve should open before the system reaches a dangerous pressure, and a corroded valve may not open when needed. Whole set, one visit.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Las Vegas

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. The connection height and size usually match. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, since many original tanks were undersized from the start. Check before swapping.

    Residential Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Las Vegas

    Most homes on a well have a tank between 20 and 86 gallons sitting near the pressure switch. First step, kill the breaker to the pump. Then we drain the system, unthread the old tank from the tank tee, set the pre-charge on the new one, and connect it with new fittings and fresh sealant. Usually a morning.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Las Vegas, NM

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. You get even pressure across fixtures. These systems can run on a smaller tank, though the drive and wiring add to the price. We explain the trade-off.
