    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Las Vegas

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. If your laundry or utility sink sits below the sewer line, its drain runs to a sink pump or a sewage ejector, and we check the check valve and the discharge line when we replace the trap. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    P-Trap Replacement in Las Vegas

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust from the inside, the plating flakes off, and a slip nut that will not loosen often twists the whole trap apart, so we cut it out and install heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches the room. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Choosing Drain Material in Las Vegas, NM

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It will not rust and costs less, while thick brass is worth it where the trap shows, like under a pedestal. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Patching one piece rarely works. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Pop-Up Drain Assembly Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    A stopper that drops on its own has a worn pivot. When the pivot ball wears and the nut stops sealing, the stopper drops or sticks and the nut drips. A new pop-up fixes all of it. Takes about an hour.

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Las Vegas

    Lift-rod pop-ups are the most common bath drain. The old body comes out, the seat gets cleaned, and the new flange goes in sealed with a clevis set for easy lifting. We match the faucet finish. Overflow-style drains are standard.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Las Vegas, NM

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. They clean easier too. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Commercial Lavatory Drain Replacement in Las Vegas

    Public restroom drains take constant use. We replace them with offset grid drains and heavy-duty traps, and where accessibility rules apply we add offset tailpieces and insulated trap covers so a wheelchair user does not touch hot or sharp pipe. Work can happen after hours. Inspectors get a clean, labeled job.
