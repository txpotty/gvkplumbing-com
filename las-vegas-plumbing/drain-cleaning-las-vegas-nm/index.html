    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Cleaning in Las Vegas

    The cost of drain cleaning in Las Vegas usually ranges from $150 to $500, depending on the severity and location of the clog. More complex issues like main line blockages requiring hydro jetting or camera inspection might cost between $400 and $800. We always provide a written estimate before starting any work.

    Hydro jetting is best for tough, persistent clogs, especially those caused by heavy grease buildup, mineral deposits, or significant tree root intrusion. It provides a more complete cleaning than traditional snaking, scouring the entire pipe interior.

    Most standard drain cleaning jobs, like clearing a kitchen or bathroom sink, can be finished within 1 to 2 hours. More complex main line blockages or jobs requiring hydro jetting might take 2 to 4 hours, depending on access and severity.

    Most fixture drains are cleared in 30 to 60 minutes. Main line jobs usually take 1 to 2 hours, longer if roots are heavy or there is no cleanout.

    A plunger or a small hand auger can handle a simple hair clog near the drain. For deep clogs, main line backups or repeat problems, a pro has the machines and camera to find and correct the real cause.

    Water softeners and filtration systems are popular around Las Vegas because they extend the life of fixtures and cut down on spotting. Mineral scale inside pipes leaves a rough surface that traps grease, soap and hair. Roots also push into sewer lines looking for water, particularly during dry stretches.

    We usually advise against chemical drain cleaners. While they might offer a temporary fix, they can corrode pipes, especially older ones, and are often useless against tough clogs like tree roots or heavy grease buildup. They can also pose health risks.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling noises from pipes, foul odors coming from drains, standing water in sinks or showers, and water backing up into other fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is time to call a plumber.
