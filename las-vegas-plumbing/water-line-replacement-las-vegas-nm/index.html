    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Las Vegas, NM

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in Las Vegas, NM

    Street pressure varies a lot from one neighborhood to the next. Plumbing code caps house pressure at 80 psi. If your new line comes in above that, we add a pressure reducing valve right after the main shutoff, set it around 55 to 65 psi, and replace the gate valve with a quarter-turn ball valve. A quarter turn and the water is off.

    Copper Service Line Installation in Las Vegas, NM

    Copper is still the choice for some homeowners. It has a long track record. We flare or use brass compression fittings at the ends, keep the run in one piece between them, and pad it with sand to keep stones off.     Burst Main Line Response in Las Vegas, NM

    A burst main is a fast-moving problem.     PEX Water Line Replacement in Las Vegas

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster, which matters on a long run. Scale and corrosion are not a problem with it. We use PEX-a or PEX rated for direct burial, use only fittings listed for burial, and lay tracer wire on top so a locator can find it later, since plastic pipe does not show up on a metal detector. Cheap insurance.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Las Vegas

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. Street pressure does not bother it.     PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Las Vegas
