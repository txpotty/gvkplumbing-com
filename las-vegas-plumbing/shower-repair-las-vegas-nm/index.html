    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Las Vegas, NM

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Las Vegas

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. A new cartridge fixes it. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Leaky Shower Valve Repair in Las Vegas

    One drip every few seconds adds up to hundreds of gallons a year.     Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Las Vegas, NM

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. A hand auger through the strainer clears most of them, and a drum machine handles clogs that have moved past the trap. Chemical cleaners rarely reach it. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Las Vegas

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.
