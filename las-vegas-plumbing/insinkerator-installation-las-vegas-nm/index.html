    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Las Vegas

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty.     Air Switch Disposal Installation in Las Vegas

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube.
