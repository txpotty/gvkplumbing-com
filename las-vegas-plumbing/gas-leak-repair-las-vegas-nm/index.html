    Underground Gas Line Repair in Las Vegas

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen.     Gas Line Repair & Replacement in Las Vegas, NM

    Black iron pipe with threaded fittings is still the most common interior gas pipe, and the leak is nearly always at a thread, not in the middle of a length.     Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Las Vegas, NM

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.
