    Basement Wall Sealing in Las Vegas, NM

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Evaporative coolers are common here, and their supply lines, float valves and drain lines need seasonal maintenance to avoid leaks. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then seal the face with a masonry waterproofer that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. Most homeowners notice the musty smell is gone within a few weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Las Vegas

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Caliche, a hard, cement-like soil layer, is common in New Mexico and can make digging for sewer and water line repairs difficult work. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Las Vegas

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. They can mean the wall is bowing from soil pressure, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Las Vegas, NM

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Las Vegas, NM

    Houses built before modern drainage rules often have no footing drain at all, or a clay tile drain that collapsed decades ago.     Foundation Crack Repair in Las Vegas
