    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Carlsbad

    Polybutylene's built-in weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Water softeners and filtration systems are popular around Carlsbad because they lengthen the life of fixtures and cut down on spotting. Hard water can also add stress to pipe that is already breaking down.
