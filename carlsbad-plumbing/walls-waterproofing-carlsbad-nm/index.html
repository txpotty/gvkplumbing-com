    Wall Waterproofing Work We Do in Carlsbad, NM

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Carlsbad

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. Wet insulation comes out, and fans dry the cavity before we close it up.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Carlsbad

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Carlsbad, NM

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer with a line to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. The wall behind them can take a moisture-resistant board and sealer.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Carlsbad

    Housing in New Mexico ranges from historic adobe homes to newer stucco subdivisions, and plumbing materials vary just as widely. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and install drain tile where water pools along the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Carlsbad

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, cement board with a membrane or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Drainage System Integration in Carlsbad, NM

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Carlsbad, NM

    Concrete block is porous, and its hollow cores fill with water like a sponge. Evaporative coolers are common here, and their supply lines, float valves and drain lines need seasonal maintenance to avoid leaks. We drill weep holes at the bottom course to drain the cores, then coat the face with a masonry waterproofing product that lets vapor out but stops liquid water. The musty smell usually fades within weeks. The weep holes empty into interior drain tile when there is one.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Carlsbad, NM

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head aimed at the house instead of the bed, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.
