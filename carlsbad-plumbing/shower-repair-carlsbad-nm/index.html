    Fixing Showers in Carlsbad, NM

    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Carlsbad

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. The trim comes off first. It is usually a loose bonnet nut or a worn O-ring.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Carlsbad

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. We clear and test the drain. Loose drains get resealed, and cracked ones get replaced.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Carlsbad

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. If the head drips with the valve closed, water is slipping past the cartridge, and a new head will not change that. We fix the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Carlsbad, NM

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. Because everything is linked, a weak diverter starves the head and a leaky valve drips from the spout and the head alike. We check all of it. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Hot Water Pressure Problems in Carlsbad, NM

    Weak hot water with strong cold usually rules out the showerhead. It comes down to the hot side, whether that is a sediment-choked heater outlet or a cartridge with scale packed in the hot port. Each side gets tested. Good hot flow at the sinks points us straight at the shower cartridge.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Carlsbad, NM

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Same-Day Shower Leak Repair in Carlsbad, NM

    Shut off the main first if water is getting where it should not. A sudden leak behind the wall or a valve that will not close can soak framing fast, so the main valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, is your first stop. Then call us. We stop the leak, make the repair or a safe temporary fix and check for anything that needs drying.
