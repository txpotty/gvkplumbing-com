    Water Heater and Appliance Leak Repair in Carlsbad, NM

    Water heaters, softeners and laundry machines are often installed in basements, and their leaks pool quickly. A dripping T&P valve, a corroded tank bottom or a split washer hose are common culprits. We repair or replace the failed part and install drain pans and leak sensors to catch future problems early. If a tank has rusted through, we can replace it the same day in most situations.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Carlsbad

    Water lines that run along basement ceilings and walls can leak at fittings, valves or pinholes. Water softeners and filtration systems are popular around Carlsbad because they extend the life of fixtures and cut down on spotting. Water chemistry affects how fast copper corrodes, and older galvanized lines often rust from the inside. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section with copper or PEX and pressure test the repair. If the line shows widespread wear, we will tell you whether a partial repipe makes more sense.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Carlsbad

    When water enters through foundation walls or the slab joint, the cause is most often surface water pooling against the house.
