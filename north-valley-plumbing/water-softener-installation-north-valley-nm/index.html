    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in North Valley, NM

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. GVK Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.
