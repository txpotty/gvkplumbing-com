    Water Line Replacement in North Valley, NM

    A leaking main water line can lead to significant water loss, high bills and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks or low water pressure. We make sure of a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, bringing back reliable water flow.

    Water Softener Replacement in North Valley, NM

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries naturally occurring arsenic or high mineral content that calls for testing and treatment. If your old water softener is no longer properly removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide truly soft water throughout your North Valley home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Replacement in North Valley

    Costs vary by job. A tank water heater replacement usually runs $1,400 to $3,000, a water softener $1,500 to $3,500, a water line $1,800 to $5,500 and a sewer line $4,000 to $15,000 or more.

    For water heater replacements, we primarily recommend and install reliable brands such as Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White and Navien. These manufacturers are known for their durability, energy efficiency and strong warranty coverage, providing long-term value for your North Valley home.

    Most tank water heater replacements take two to four hours. Code upgrades or relocations can take longer.

    While some minor plumbing repairs can be DIY, major replacements like water heaters, sewer lines or repiping should always be handled by licensed professionals. These jobs involve complex systems, potential safety hazards (gas, high water pressure) and strict code compliance. Improper installation can lead to leaks, damage and even severe safety risks, costing more in the long run.

    We install water heaters from Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith, tankless units from Rinnai and Navien, and quality softeners and well pumps from trusted manufacturers.

    Consider the age of the equipment, the cost of repair compared with replacement and how often it has needed service. We give you both options with pricing so you can choose.

    Climate, freezes and soil type all shape how long plumbing lasts. Freeze-thaw cycles can damage pipes, and corrosive or shifting soils can speed up pipe degradation. Expansive clay soils in some areas swell and shrink with moisture, which can crack slab foundations and the pipes that run beneath them.
