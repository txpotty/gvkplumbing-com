    Pipe Bursting Services in North Valley, NM

    Bursting replaces a pipe that is too far gone to line. The old pipe gets split. As a winch drags the head through, the old pipe fractures into the soil and new HDPE is pulled into the same path behind it. HDPE does not rust or leak at joints in North Valley, NM soil.

    Cured-in-Place Pipe Lining in North Valley

    The old pipe gets cleaned first. After that, we insert the liner. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy resin is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or UV light, and once it hardens, it forms a smooth, jointless pipe inside the old one.     Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in North Valley

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. Once inside the cleaned pipe, it gets inflated against the wall and cured with heat or UV, forming a new pipe with no joints. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your North Valley yard.
