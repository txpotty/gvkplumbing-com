    PEX Water Line Repair in North Valley, NM

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a flexible, durable, and corrosion-resistant option more and more used in homes across North Valley, NM. While PEX is less prone to leaks than older materials, damage can still occur from improper installation, rodent chewing, or impact.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in North Valley

    Many water line problems are made worse by high incoming pressure. A failed pressure reducing valve can push well over 80 psi into your plumbing, which stresses the service line and every fixture. We test static pressure with a gauge and replace worn regulators from brands like Watts and Zurn Wilkins. We also swap seized gate valves for full-port ball valves so you can shut the water off fast in an emergency.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in North Valley

We'll check whether your water line issue is a good candidate for trenchless technology.
