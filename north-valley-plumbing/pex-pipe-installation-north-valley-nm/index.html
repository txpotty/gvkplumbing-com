    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in North Valley, NM

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is notorious for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Many insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to sell and insure.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in North Valley, NM

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in North Valley

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can drastically improve your plumbing system. We remove outdated galvanized, copper or polybutylene pipes and replace them with durable PEX, upgrading your entire water delivery system. This prevents future leaks, improves water pressure and eliminates the rust-colored water often seen with older piping in North Valley.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in North Valley

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in North Valley, NM

    Expanding your home or remodeling a kitchen or bathroom often requires new plumbing lines. PEX is an excellent choice for these projects in North Valley, NM due to its adaptability and ease of installation in existing wall cavities or new structures. We can seamlessly tie new PEX lines into your current system, ensuring proper water supply to new fixtures and appliances without compromising your existing plumbing.
