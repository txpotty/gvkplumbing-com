    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in North Valley

    For the most stubborn sewer line clogs, hydro jetting is the top choice. This powerful technique uses high-pressure water streams to blast away grease, scale, mineral buildup, and even tree roots from the interior walls of your sewer pipes. GVK Plumbing employs professional hydro jetting equipment for sewer cleaning in North Valley, delivering a thorough cleaning that leaves your pipes virtually spotless, greatly reducing the chance of future blockages and extending the life of your sewer system.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in North Valley, NM

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Homes built in the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail and are worth replacing. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in North Valley

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines gently and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you plan a replacement.
