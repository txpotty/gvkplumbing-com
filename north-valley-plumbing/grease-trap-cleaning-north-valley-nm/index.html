    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Lines in North Valley, NM

    A cable machine pokes a hole through grease. Jetting actually removes it. Our jetters run around 3,000 to 4,000 psi through a spinning nozzle, which scours the whole inside of the pipe and pushes the loosened grease downstream. A camera pass afterward shows whether the line is really clean.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in North Valley, NM

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two.
