    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in North Valley, NM

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. The bond is a heavy copper wire tying the gas pipe to the house ground. Plenty of older installs lack it.     Underground Gas Line Repair in North Valley

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen.     Outdoor Gas Line Repair in North Valley

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in North Valley, NM
