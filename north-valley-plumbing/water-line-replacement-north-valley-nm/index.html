    Copper Water Line Installation in North Valley

    Type K copper is the thick-wall grade made for burial. It handles high street pressure without complaint. We bed it in sand, keep it away from sharp rock, and sleeve it where it passes through a foundation wall, because concrete and copper rubbing together for twenty years eventually wear a hole. PEX is cheaper.

    PEX Water Line Installation in North Valley, NM

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house. Freeze risk in New Mexico depends heavily on elevation, with northern and mountain areas seeing hard freezes every winter and lower desert areas seeing fewer. PEX can swell a little in a freeze and go back to shape, where copper splits. We still bury it below the frost depth the local code sets, and we keep it out of direct sun at the meter box, since UV light breaks it down. Short exposure only.

    Commercial Water Line Replacement in North Valley

    Larger buildings bring larger pipe, deeper trenches and more inspection steps. Backflow testing and the report to the water provider are part of the job.     PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in North Valley

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, since a single coil reaches all the way without a splice. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.
