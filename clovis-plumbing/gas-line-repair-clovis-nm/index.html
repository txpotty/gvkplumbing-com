    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Clovis

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils in some areas swell and shrink with moisture, which can shift slab foundations and the pipes that run beneath them. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Clovis

Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    CSST Repair in Clovis
