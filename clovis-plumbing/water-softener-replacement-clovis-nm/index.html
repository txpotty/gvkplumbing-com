    Picking the Right Softener for Clovis, NM Homes

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Clovis

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Salt-Free Alternatives in Clovis, NM

    Some homeowners do not want salt or have no drain near the main line. In those cases a TAC conditioner works, though we are upfront that it stops scale and does not make water feel soft. Sodium concerns are another reason.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Clovis

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Clovis, NM

    Swapping a salt-based softener in New Mexico homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. Every joint gets checked for leaks under pressure. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Clovis

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Upsizing a Whole-Home Softener in Clovis

    Undersized softeners are common in homes that added a bathroom or a couple of teenagers since the original system went in. A 32,000-grain unit that regenerated weekly for a family of three may be running dry every three days now. Sizing is based on current usage.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Clovis

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. You will buy less salt. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Clovis, NM

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. For a small house on moderately hard water, a basic metered single-tank unit often does the job for much less than a twin-tank system. You see the choices on one page.
