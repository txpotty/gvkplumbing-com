    Sump Pump Maintenance in Clovis, NM Ahead of Storm Season

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Clovis, NM

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Plenty of crawlspaces and deep window wells use a small pump to clear storm runoff. Our plumber clears the screen, tests the float and traces the discharge to daylight or an approved drain, since a line that ends under the house only relocates the water. Even in milder parts of the state, clear winter nights can drop below freezing, so outdoor spigots and evaporative cooler lines need draining each fall.

    Pre-Storm Season Pump Testing in Clovis

    Summer monsoon storms can bring sudden heavy rain to New Mexico, causing flash flooding that overwhelms drainage and backs up into low areas. A few weeks ahead of the wet season, we run a hose into the pit until the float trips and the pump starts. Then we time how fast it pumps out and listen to the bearings. A struggling pump can then be replaced on a calm day instead of in the middle of a storm.

    Zoeller and Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Clovis

    Brand matters less than people think, but parts do not cross over cleanly between Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty and Little Giant. Our plumber reads the model number off the label and orders the matching float or seal kit. A submersible Zoeller and a pedestal Wayne need different checks. We know both.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Clovis

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Clovis, NM

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. They need checking more often than anything in a house. We test the controls, the alarm panel and both pumps in a duplex setup, then log the amp draw on each one so trends show up over time. Downtime costs money.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Clovis, NM

    Grinding, clicking and a hum with no water moving each point to a different failure. Our plumber works from the outlet and cord to the float and then the motor. A tripped GFCI is a quick fix. A seized motor means a new pump, and we keep replacements on the truck so the pit is never left unprotected overnight.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Clovis, NM

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Annual Sump Pump Inspections in Clovis

    Once a year is the minimum. We check the motor, impeller, float and discharge line on every annual visit. After that, we fill the basin and time the drawdown, since a slow pump-out is the first hint of a tired impeller or a restricted line.
