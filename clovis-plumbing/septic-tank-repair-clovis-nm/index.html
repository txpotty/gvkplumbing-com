    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Clovis, NM

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Clovis

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Each lid we install gets screws or a lock so a child cannot lift it.

    Septic Tee Repair in Clovis

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Clovis

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and the water that comes back tells us how bad it is. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Commercial Septic Repair in Clovis, NM

    Churches and small offices on septic run more water through the tank than any house, and grease from a kitchen makes it worse. Repairs get sized to real daily flow instead of house numbers.     Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Clovis

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Press the silence button and cut back on water. Our tech confirms the pump runs and looks for a float snagged on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.
