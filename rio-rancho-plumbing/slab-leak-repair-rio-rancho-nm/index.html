    Slab Leak Detection in Rio Rancho

    We start by shutting off fixtures and watching the meter to confirm a leak. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to determine which one is leaking. Using acoustic ground microphones, we listen for the sound of escaping water through the concrete. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor surface.

    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Rio Rancho, NM

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section.     Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Rio Rancho, NM

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat expands and contracts the pipe against concrete and gravel. Mineral scale and constant recirculation can wear the pipe wall faster. We repair the leak or reroute the hot line, and we can adjust recirculation pumps to reduce wear. Warm floors and higher gas or electric bills are common signs.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Rio Rancho

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Rio Rancho with aging copper or galvanized pipes.
