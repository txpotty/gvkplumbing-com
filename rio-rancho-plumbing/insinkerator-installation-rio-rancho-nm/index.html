    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Rio Rancho

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Hard Water and Disposals in Rio Rancho

    Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. Scale builds up on grind rings and drain tailpieces over the years. A scaled unit gets loud. Drainage slows too, since crust narrows the tailpiece and trap, and we check those parts before tying a new unit into them. Heavily scaled parts get replaced the same visit.

    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Rio Rancho, NM
