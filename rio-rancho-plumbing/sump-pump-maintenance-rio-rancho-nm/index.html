    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Rio Rancho, NM

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. A tethered float can tangle on the pump body or catch on the side of the basin. Vertical floats stick when mineral crust builds up on the rod. For narrow basins where a float keeps catching, an electronic switch with no moving float is a good swap that ends the problem.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Rio Rancho, NM

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Any brand can rattle its discharge fittings loose over time. Easy fix.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Rio Rancho

    The check valve sits on the discharge pipe a foot or so above the pump and keeps the water in the line from falling back into the pit. When it fails, the pump pumps the same water twice. Motors wear out faster. We test the valve, look for a weep hole between the pump and the valve, and check every joint up to the wall.

    Submersible Sump Pump Maintenance in Rio Rancho

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. Our plumber pulls the unit out so we can see the cord and the shaft seal. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Rio Rancho, NM

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.
