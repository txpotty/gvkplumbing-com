    How We Fix Basement Leaks in Rio Rancho, NM

    Interior Drainage Systems in Rio Rancho

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Water then runs behind it into the channel.

    After-Hours Basement Leak Service in Rio Rancho, NM

    Basement leaks rarely wait for business hours. Tanks tend to split at night when nobody is watching. The on-call truck carries couplings, valves and a wet vac, so the first visit usually stops the leak and restores water to the rest of the house.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Rio Rancho

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Basement Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Rio Rancho, NM

    A simple fitting or supply line repair usually costs $200 to $600. Cast iron stack work tends to land between $500 and $2,000. A new sump pit and pump runs $1,200 to $2,500, while interior perimeter drains and exterior waterproofing cost several thousand dollars more because of the concrete work and digging involved. Prices vary with access.

    Supply Line Leak Repair in Rio Rancho

    Water lines along a basement ceiling tend to leak at fittings, valves and pinholes. Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. We cut out the bad section and replace it with copper or PEX, and if we find more than two or three pinholes on one run, we recommend repiping that run instead of patching it again. Repiping a basement ceiling is usually a one-day job.

    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Rio Rancho, NM

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing, cleaning the wall, and applying a membrane with a drainage board over it. It is the most thorough fix and the most disruptive one. Plan on several days. We bring in a waterproofing partner for the membrane and handle the footing drain and its tie-in to a sump or daylight outlet ourselves.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Rio Rancho

    Hairline cracks in a poured wall are common and often stay dry, but a crack that seeps after rain needs attention. Expansive clay soils in some areas swell and shrink with moisture, which can shift slab foundations and the pipes that run beneath them. Polyurethane injection from the inside fills a leaking crack all the way through and flexes with the wall. Most injections take an hour or two.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Rio Rancho, NM

    Damp walls without a crack usually mean water moving through porous concrete or block. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.
