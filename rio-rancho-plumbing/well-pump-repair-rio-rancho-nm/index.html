    Jet Pump System Repair in Rio Rancho, NM

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. GVK Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Rio Rancho operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Rio Rancho

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are common causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most affordable well repairs.

    Diagnosing Low Water Pressure in Rio Rancho

    Pressure Tank Repair in Rio Rancho, NM

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps prematurely. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank. Correct tank sizing protects the pump and gives more even pressure at the tap.
