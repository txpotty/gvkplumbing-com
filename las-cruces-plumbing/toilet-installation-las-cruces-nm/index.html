    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Las Cruces, NM

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low.     Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Las Cruces, NM

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Las Cruces

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. Standard two-piece toilets ride on our trucks.     New Construction Toilet Installation in Las Cruces

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Las Cruces

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Each one has its own parts quirks. Some one-piece TOTO models mount with a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler toilets use a tank-to-bowl gasket that most hardware stores do not carry, so we stock both. We keep those parts on hand.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Las Cruces, NM

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. For a toilet that means a 3 inch drain with a vent, plus a cold supply stub. The drain goes 12 inches out from the finished wall line, which means we add the drywall thickness to the framing measurement. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Las Cruces

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Las Cruces

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. The carrier holds the whole load, so its feet get anchored to slab or framing and it gets plumbed before any wall finish goes on. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Las Cruces

    Setting a customer-supplied standard toilet usually costs $200 to $400 in labor. With a mid-range toilet included, most installs land between $450 and $900. A wall-hung toilet with its carrier runs $1,500 to $3,500 or more, since the wall must be opened and refinished. We quote a flat price.

    Figure one to two hours for a standard floor-mount toilet. Some jobs run a full day. Wall-hung carriers and slab relocations open walls or floors, so they get booked as separate projects with cure time built in.

    A one-piece toilet is cast as a single unit, so there is no tank-to-bowl joint to leak. Expect a higher price and more weight. A two-piece bolts the tank to the bowl with a rubber gasket, which makes it lighter to carry up stairs and cheaper to fix, since you can replace just the tank or just the bowl if one cracks. Both flush well.

    It is a toilet with the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly chair height, compared with 15 inches or so on a standard bowl. Most adults find it easier on the knees. Children and shorter people may find their feet dangle, so for a family bath a standard height with a grab bar can be the better call.
