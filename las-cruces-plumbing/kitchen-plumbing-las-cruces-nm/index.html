    Disposal Jams and Hums in Las Cruces, NM

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. That is often ten minutes. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before we test it with water. No sound at all points us to the switch, the outlet or the breaker.

    Grease Trap Repair in Las Cruces, NM

    Cracked lids, missing baffles and rusted inlet tees all let grease pass straight through a trap. We inspect each part during cleaning and replace what has failed, in most cases right then.     Disposal Repair and Replacement in Las Cruces, NM

    A disposal that hums but will not spin usually has something wedged in it.
