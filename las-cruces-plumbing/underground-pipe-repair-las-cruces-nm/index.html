    Frequently Asked Questions About Underground Pipe Repair in Las Cruces

    A single repair on a buried water line typically costs $800 to $2,500 including locating and excavation. Full main water service replacement typically runs $2,500 to $6,500, and sewer lateral repairs range from $1,500 to $5,000 depending on depth.

    Shallow irrigation leaks are sometimes a reasonable DIY repair. Main water lines and sewer laterals require locating, utility marking and code-compliant fittings, so a licensed plumber is the safer choice.

    Small repairs often do not need a permit, but full water service or sewer line replacement usually does. Work in the public right-of-way always requires coordination with the city. We handle permits when needed.

    For water lines we typically use PEX or Type K copper, both approved for direct burial. For sewer lines we use Schedule 40 PVC. The right choice depends on the existing pipe and local code.

    GVK Plumbing uses high-quality, industry-approved materials from reputable brands known for durability and longevity. This includes PEX piping from manufacturers like Uponor or Viega, and various PVC and ABS pipe products that meet ASTM standards.

    GVK Plumbing stands behind the quality of our underground pipe repair work. We offer a solid warranty on parts and labor for all our underground pipe services, giving you peace of mind. Warranty details will be provided with your repair estimate, so you understand the protection you receive.

    We use acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of escaping water, tracer gas that rises through soil at the leak point and electronic locators that trace the pipe path. Together these narrow the leak to a small area.

    The duration of an underground pipe repair depends heavily on the severity and location of the damage, as well as the repair method chosen. Minor repairs using trenchless techniques might be completed in a single day. More extensive issues, such as full sewer line replacements requiring excavation, could take two to five days, including restoration work.
