    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Las Cruces

    Pull-down spray heads take a beating. After a few years, the hose wears thin, the spray button sticks and the head will not dock the way it did. We swap the whole faucet. We clip the new hose weight at the right height and connect fresh braided supplies to both stops.

    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Las Cruces

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. Sometimes a new hose is enough. If the body is also worn, the whole faucet gets replaced along with the supplies.

    Delta Faucet Replacement in Las Cruces, NM

    Delta faucets come in a few cartridge families, and the model number tells you which.
