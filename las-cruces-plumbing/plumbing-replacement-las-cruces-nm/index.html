    Well Pump and Pressure Tank Replacement in Las Cruces, NM

    Rural properties near Las Cruces, NM depend on the well pump for every drop, so a failure is an emergency. Watch for rising and falling pressure, sputtering taps, nonstop running or a breaker that trips, since each points to the pump, switch or tank. Pumps rarely recover once this starts.

    Sump Pump and Well Pump Replacement in Las Cruces, NM

    A dead sump pump on a wet night means water on the floor, and a dead well pump means no water at all. In Las Cruces, NM, we replace sump pumps with cast iron or thermoplastic models sized for your inflow, add a check valve, and recommend a battery backup, while well pumps get matched to depth, flow and the pressure switch setting. Both get tested before we go.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Las Cruces

    A well pump that clicks on and off constantly usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. A new tank fixes it. We also check the switch.

    Pipe Replacement and Repiping in Las Cruces

    Corroded, leaking or galvanized pipe in Las Cruces leads to rusty water and expensive leaks. We replace failing sections or repipe the whole house with PEX or copper, plan routes that keep wall openings small and finish every job with a pressure test held long enough to show even a slow drip at a fitting. Patching is included.
