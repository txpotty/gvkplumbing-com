    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Las Cruces

    A softener using far too much salt or water usually has settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units may regenerate on a schedule regardless of use. We retest hardness, reprogram the valve or upgrade the head to a metered control. Proper settings can significantly cut salt and water use.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Las Cruces

    If hard water symptoms return, the softener may not be regenerating. Causes include a failed timer motor, a bad circuit board, a stuck meter turbine or incorrect settings after a power outage. We test each component, replace the failed part and program the valve for your household size and hardness. You get soft water again by the next cycle.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Las Cruces, NM

    Deciding whether to repair or replace your water softener can be a hard choice. GVK Plumbing provides honest, expert advice based on the age of your system, the extent of the damage, and the cost-effectiveness of repairs. We'll give you a clear breakdown of options, helping you make the best decision for your budget and long-term water quality needs in Las Cruces, NM.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Las Cruces, NM

    A salt bridge, a crust of salt that forms in the brine tank, can prevent your water softener from regenerating properly. This leads to hard water throughout your home. Our team can safely break up and remove salt bridges, clean the brine tank, and advise on best practices to prevent future occurrences. We also repair leaks or damage to the brine tank itself, common in older systems.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Las Cruces, NM

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates suction to draw brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. When it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying. We clean or replace the injector and screen and check the brine line for leaks.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Las Cruces, NM

    Leaks in a water softener can range from minor drips around fittings to significant puddles from a cracked tank or faulty valve. These leaks can cause water damage and waste water. Our plumbers quickly pinpoint the source of leaks, whether from seals, O-rings, hoses, or internal components, and perform durable repairs. We use appropriate sealing compounds and replacement parts for a lasting fix in Las Cruces, NM.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Las Cruces

    Over time, the resin beads in your water softener can become fouled or degrade, losing their ability to exchange ions and soften water. This is a frequent issue, especially with older systems or those exposed to high levels of chlorine. GVK Plumbing provides professional resin bed replacement services, properly removing old resin and installing new, high-quality media to restore your softener's efficiency and capacity in Las Cruces.
