    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Las Cruces

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Las Cruces, NM

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. They make matching backup units. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Las Cruces

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Las Cruces, NM

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Access is often tight. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Las Cruces

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Las Cruces

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Las Cruces

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. For those jobs we install duplex systems where two pumps alternate, with a control panel and a high water alarm that can tie into the building monitoring. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. Work is scheduled around your operating hours.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Las Cruces, NM

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. You can reach the motor and the switch without fishing anything out of the pit, which helps in a basement where the pit is shallow or tight. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.
