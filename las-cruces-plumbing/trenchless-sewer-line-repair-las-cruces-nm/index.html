    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Las Cruces, NM

    Sewer lines in Las Cruces, NM can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or misalign. Caliche, a hard, cement-like soil layer, is common in New Mexico and can make digging for sewer and water line repairs difficult work. Tree root intrusion is another major culprit, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also contribute to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Las Cruces

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is best for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Las Cruces that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Las Cruces, NM

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral often runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Traditional excavation can be cheaper upfront on short, shallow runs, but restoring concrete and landscaping can quickly close the gap.     Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Las Cruces

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is crucial. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This allows us to precisely diagnose the problem, identify its exact location and determine the most appropriate trenchless method, whether it is pipe bursting or CIPP. This diagnostic step makes for a precise and lasting repair in Las Cruces.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Las Cruces

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Costs in Las Cruces, NM

    While the upfront cost of trenchless sewer repair in Las Cruces, NM can sometimes be comparable to or slightly higher than traditional methods, the overall savings are often significant. This is because trenchless repairs eliminate the need for extensive excavation, backfilling and landscape restoration. Homeowners save money on repairing driveways, patios and gardens, making it a more economical choice in the long run for many.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Las Cruces

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Las Cruces homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Las Cruces, NM

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every couple of months.
