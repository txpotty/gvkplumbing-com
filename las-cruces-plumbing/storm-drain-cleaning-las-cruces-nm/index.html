    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Las Cruces, NM

    Dry wells let storm water soak into the ground instead of flowing to a street. They are found on commercial sites, HOA common areas and some residential lots. Silt slowly seals the settling chamber and the drainage rock, slowing how fast water drains. We vacuum the chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet, and check drain-down time. If the dry well no longer drains properly, we explain options for rehabilitation or replacement.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Las Cruces, NM

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Las Cruces, NM. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is highly effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    We use water jetters, vacuum equipment and push cameras built for drain work. These tools remove debris without crushing thin-wall storm pipe.

    Water pooling around grates, overflowing downspouts, standing water against walls and mud stains near inlets are typical signs.

    It is usually recommended to have your storm drains inspected and cleaned at least once a year. Properties with many trees or those that see frequent heavy rains may need twice-yearly cleaning to prevent buildup and blockages.

    We use a range of professional-grade equipment, including powerful hydro jetting machines that use high-pressure water to scour pipes, drain snakes with various cutting heads for root removal, and waterproof camera inspection systems to accurately diagnose issues and confirm thorough cleaning.

    Cleaning does not need a permit. New dry wells or major drainage changes may require permits or registration, and we can help you.
