    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Farmington, NM

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. They adjust flow when a toilet flushes so the mix holds, and thermostatic valves hold an exact temperature. Either one satisfies scald rules. Thermostatic costs more.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Farmington

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. We find galvanized supplies that rust shut, valves soldered to copper with no stops anywhere, and spouts threaded onto steel nipples that snap when you try to turn them. Adapters for old pipe ride along. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Farmington

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Tub Spout Replacement in Farmington

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. The connection gets checked first. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Farmington, NM

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Farmington

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. Single-handle valves can often be updated from the front, while three-handle valves usually get cut out and replaced. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Farmington

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Corroded Tub Faucet Replacement in Farmington, NM

    Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. Corrosion shows up as green crust at the handle or a stem you cannot turn by hand. By that point the threads that hold the trim are often too weak to grip a new part, and the valve body itself may be pitted where the cartridge seals against it. Replacement is the better call.
