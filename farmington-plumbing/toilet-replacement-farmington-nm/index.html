    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Farmington, NM

    Toilets made before 1994 often use 3.5 gallons a flush, and some from the 1970s use five or more.     Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Farmington, NM

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half.     Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Farmington

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Farmington, NM

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models.     Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Farmington

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Farmington, NM

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. Work gets scheduled around your business hours. We match the bowl to the gallons per flush on the valve, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Farmington, NM

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.
