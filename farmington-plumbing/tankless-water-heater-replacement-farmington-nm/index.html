    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Farmington

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Farmington

    Sizing a tankless starts with how many fixtures run at once and how cold the incoming water is, not the square footage of the house. Two showers and a dishwasher at the same time can need 7 or 8 gallons per minute. The unit is chosen from that figure. We handle the gas, water and vent changes needed to bring the old installation up to current code.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Farmington, NM

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Farmington

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Swaps take an hour or two.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Farmington

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Farmington, NM

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Many panels cannot spare that. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.
