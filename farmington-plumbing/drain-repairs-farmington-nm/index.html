    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Farmington

    The cost of drain repairs in Farmington, NM varies widely depending on the nature and severity of the problem. Simple clog removals might range from $150 to $400, while more complex issues like sewer line repairs could be anywhere from $800 to $5,000 or more, especially if excavation is required. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after diagnosis.

    Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more cleaning.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, effectively removing stubborn clogs, grease buildup and even tree roots. It's usually used when traditional snaking isn't enough, or for preventive maintenance on main sewer lines, especially in older homes with buildup issues.

    A dry trap, a cracked drain fitting, a failed wax ring or a blocked vent can all let sewer gas into a room. We locate the source with a smoke test or visual inspection and fix it.

    We work with all standard drain pipe materials found in New Mexico homes, including PVC, ABS, cast iron and copper. Our repairs and replacements use high-quality, code-compliant materials from trusted manufacturers for durability and longevity.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or hide a larger problem.

    Most trap and fixture drain repairs take one to two hours. Replacing drain pipe inside a wall or under a floor can take half a day to a full day. We give you a time estimate when we quote the job.
