    French Drain Installation in Farmington

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after heavy rain. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or approved discharge point.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Farmington, NM

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send storm runoff directly into a garage or under a door. A channel drain with a grate catches that sheet flow before it reaches the building. We use durable polymer or concrete channels from brands like NDS and set them flush with the surface. The channel ties into solid pipe that carries the water to a proper outlet.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Farmington, NM

    A blocked main sewer line can cause widespread backups throughout your home, often due to tree root intrusion or sediment buildup. Our team uses powerful equipment, including hydro-jetting, to blast through tough obstructions and thoroughly clean your main sewer line. We can also inspect the line with a camera to find the exact cause and location of the problem.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Farmington

    Outdoor drains, including storm drains and area drains, are important for preventing water accumulation around your property, especially during heavy rainfall. We clear debris, leaves, and sediment from these systems to restore proper water runoff and protect your foundation from potential water damage. Regular cleaning can prevent expensive landscape and structural issues.

    Main Line and Sewer Drainage in Farmington, NM

    When several fixtures back up at once, the problem is most often in the main line to the city sewer or septic.
