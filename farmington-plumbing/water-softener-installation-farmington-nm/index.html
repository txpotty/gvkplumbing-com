    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Farmington

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Farmington

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. We follow the Ecodyne manual for the bypass, drain and overflow connections, then program hardness and regeneration settings for your water. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Farmington, NM

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. Salt bills drop. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Farmington

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. Connections and setup included. Existing loops make it faster.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Farmington, NM

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. Disposal is included. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Farmington

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Farmington

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. We add a bypass valve so you can isolate the softener for service, and we install a pressure regulator if your street pressure runs above 80 psi. Soft water reaches every fixture. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Farmington

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. The savings add up. Several models are available.
