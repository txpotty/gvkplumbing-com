    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Farmington

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Farmington, NM

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. Undersized vents spill CO. Draft gets tested on every job.
