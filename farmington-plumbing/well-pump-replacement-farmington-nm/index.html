    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Farmington

    The cost of well pump replacement in Farmington, NM can vary widely, usually ranging from $1,500 to $5,000 or more. This price depends on factors like the type of pump, submersible or jet, well depth, horsepower and any additional components needed like pressure tanks or control boxes. We provide a written, upfront estimate after diagnosing your situation.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, usually less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has its own applications and benefits.

    In many areas, a plumbing or well permit is required for well pump replacement to ensure compliance with local building and health codes. GVK Plumbing handles all required permitting on your behalf, so your installation is fully compliant and safe.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is highly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, preventing costly mistakes.

    Common signs of a failing well pump include no water, sputtering faucets, reduced water pressure, discolored water or unusually high electricity bills. If your pump runs constantly or cycles on and off too frequently, these are also signs that replacement might be necessary.

    Permit requirements vary by county. We follow local requirements for all well work.

    Most standard well pump replacements can be finished within a single day, typically 4 to 8 hours. However, complex installations involving very deep wells, difficult access or additional system repairs may take longer. Our team works efficiently to restore your water as soon as possible.

    It is often a good idea to consider replacing your pressure tank at the same time as your well pump, especially if both are of similar age. A new pressure tank can improve the efficiency and lifespan of your new pump by reducing cycling. It also saves on labor costs to do both jobs at once.
