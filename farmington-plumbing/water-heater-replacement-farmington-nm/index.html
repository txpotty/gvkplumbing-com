    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Farmington

    Housing in New Mexico ranges from historic adobe homes to newer stucco subdivisions, and plumbing materials vary just as widely. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit.     Electric Water Heater Replacement in Farmington

    Electric water heaters in Farmington usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Farmington, NM

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option in Farmington, NM. Tankless and hybrid units cost more up front but can lower operating costs, and tankless often lasts 15 to 20 years versus about 8 to 12 for a tank. Ask for each option.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Farmington, NM

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.
