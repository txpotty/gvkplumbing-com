    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Artesia

    Many older systems have a tank that is too small for the pump, so the motor starts every time someone washes their hands. Tank size is based on drawdown, which is the available water stored between cut-out and cut-in pressure. For example, a 10 gallon per minute pump usually needs at least 10 gallons of drawdown for a one minute run time. That often means an 80 to 119 gallon tank rather than the 20 gallon unit that came with the home. Upsizing is one of the most effective ways to extend pump life.

    Well Pressure Tank Leak Repair vs. Replacement in Artesia

    When your well pressure tank shows signs of trouble, it is important to determine if a repair is feasible or if full replacement is necessary. Small air leaks or issues with the air valve might be repairable. However, if the tank itself is corroded, has a ruptured diaphragm, or is waterlogged, replacement is almost always the more cost-effective and reliable option. Our technicians in Artesia provide an straightforward assessment, explaining the pros and cons of repair versus a complete well pressure tank replacement, so you can make an informed decision.

    Tank Tee, Gauge and Relief Valve Replacement in Artesia

    The tank tee is the manifold that connects the tank, switch, gauge, relief valve and drain. Galvanized tees corrode and restrict flow, especially with sandy or mineral-heavy water.
