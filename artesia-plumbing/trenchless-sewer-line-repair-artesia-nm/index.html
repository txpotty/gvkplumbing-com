    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Artesia

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for long trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Artesia that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Emergency Trenchless Sewer Repair in Artesia

    Sewer line emergencies, such as major backups or collapses, require urgent attention. GVK Plumbing offers emergency trenchless sewer repair services in Artesia to quickly address critical issues.     Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Artesia

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We identify the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Artesia, NM

    Sewer lines in Artesia, NM can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Caliche, a hard, cement-like soil layer, is common in New Mexico and can make digging for sewer and water line repairs difficult work. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Artesia, NM

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.
