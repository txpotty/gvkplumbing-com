    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Artesia

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Most homes sit on concrete slabs, so leaks under the floor often call for slab leak detection before any repair begins. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
