    What Goes Into a Water Heater Replacement in Artesia, NM

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Artesia

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Artesia, NM

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Artesia

    Electric water heaters in Artesia usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Hot water is back the same day.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Artesia

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Recovery rate counts too.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Artesia, NM

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on the majority of our swaps. Bradford White is trade-only and durable, Rheem offers range and parts, and AO Smith brings plenty of sizes, so the pick comes down to fit, warranty and stock. Any of the three works.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Artesia

    Homes built in the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to crack and are worth replacing. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. We bring the help.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Artesia

    We use Bradford White often on our jobs because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When replacing one, we match fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty still applies, and register the new heater right away. We file the warranty.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Artesia

    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. Hot water by evening.
