    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Artesia, NM

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. It makes sense on sound pipe. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can pass the cost of repiping within a few years, so we lay out both options with real numbers and tell you which one we would pick for our own house.     Pinhole Leak Repair in Artesia

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with new copper or a PEX transition. More pinholes often follow.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Artesia

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Most take under an hour.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Artesia

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Flow drops slowly. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, so repairs on galvanized are usually short-term and replacing the run with PEX or copper is the lasting fix. Transitions get dielectric fittings.

    Underground Pipe Repair in Artesia

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, then dig a small pit at the mark or pull new pipe through the old path when the line is too far gone to patch. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Artesia, NM

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Expansive clay soils in some areas swell and shrink with moisture, which can shift slab foundations and the pipes that run beneath them. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, and you pick between a targeted floor repair and a reroute overhead. Both options get a price.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Artesia

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Rusty water is a sign. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, so we swap the failing pipe for PEX or copper with proper fittings between metals. You will notice the flow.
