    Utility Sink Faucet Replacement in Artesia

    Utility faucets in garages and laundry rooms take hard use. People hang buckets on the spout and yank hoses off the threads, which loosens the mounting and cracks the supply connections over time. We use brass-bodied replacements. The new faucet is anchored tight to the wall or the tub.

    Corroded Faucet Replacement in Artesia

    Green crust at the base means the faucet body is corroding. Once the brass under the finish is pitted, cartridges stop sealing against it, and every repair drips again within a few weeks. Replacement is the only fix. Even with fused nuts, we get it out and install a faucet with a ceramic disc cartridge.

    Kitchen Faucet Replacement in Artesia

    Kitchen faucets usually fail at the hose first. Pull-down hoses crack at the spot where they flex over the neck, and the leak runs inside the body down into the cabinet where it stays hidden. A hose kit can fix that. If the body is also worn, the whole faucet gets replaced along with the supplies.

    Outdoor Spigot Replacement in Artesia, NM

    Outdoor spigots wear out from sun, hoses and hard closing. Even in milder parts of the state, clear winter nights can drop below freezing, so outdoor spigots and evaporative cooler lines need draining each fall. Worn spigots get replaced, with frost-free models where needed and a vacuum breaker on each, which code calls for on every hose thread. The wall cavity gets a look too. A bib that leaked for months can leave wet insulation behind.

    Urgent Faucet Replacement in Artesia, NM

    Water that will not shut off at the faucet is an urgent call. Turn the shutoff under the sink clockwise, and go to the main if that valve will not budge or starts dripping. We take urgent calls. A plumber stops the flow, removes the broken faucet and installs a new one from the truck.

    Water-Saving Faucet Upgrades in Artesia

    Federal rules cap new kitchen and lav faucets at 2.2 gallons per minute. WaterSense lav faucets go lower, to 1.5 gallons per minute, and most people do not notice the difference because the aerator mixes air into the stream. The savings show on the bill. A WaterSense lav faucet is an easy swap.

    Tub and shower trim can be replaced without opening the wall. A Moen Posi-Temp or Delta Monitor valve takes a matching trim kit, which swaps the handle and the spout without touching the tile. Brand matters here. If the valve itself is old, we talk about a valve upgrade.
