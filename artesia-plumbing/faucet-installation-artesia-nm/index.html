    Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in Artesia, NM

    Wall-mount faucets start before the drywall closes. The rough-in valve gets set at a height and depth that suit your sink and the finished thickness of the tile, so the spout lands where it should. The spout and handles go on after tile. You get a clear deck with no faucet base to wipe around.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Artesia

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. We mount the control box high on the cabinet wall, away from where water collects, and power it with batteries or a plug-in adapter. Scale is hard on solenoids, and we install the inline filter when the maker calls for one. We test the sensor range last.

    Garage and Workshop Faucets in Artesia

    Workshop and garage sinks get abused. We install faucets with metal handles and a threaded spout, anchored to the wall or the tub deck so a pulled hose does not twist the supply lines. Flow is checked at full open. A slow tub drain gets its trap cleared at the same visit.

    Vessel Sink Faucets in Artesia, NM

    Vessel sinks sit high, so the faucet has to sit higher. We use either a tall deck faucet or a wall spout, sized so water hits near the drain and not the rim where it splashes onto the counter. Spout reach matters too. Too short and you bang your knuckles on the bowl.
