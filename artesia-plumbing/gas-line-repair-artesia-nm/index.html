    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Artesia

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Artesia, NM

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.
