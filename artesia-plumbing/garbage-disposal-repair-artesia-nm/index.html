    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Artesia

    A failed disposal can push water back into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We respond fast.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Artesia, NM

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. It is not a repairable part.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Artesia, NM

    Good grinding but slow draining usually means the discharge tube or the trap arm is packed. Rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We pull the trap, clear the tube and the arm with the right size cable, and check that the arm has at least a quarter inch of fall per foot toward the wall.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Artesia

    If a disposal is completely dead, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring, and we also test air switches and batch-feed lids that use a magnet to start the motor. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Artesia

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Failing bearings are not worth fixing.

    Jammed Disposal Repair in Artesia

    A jam usually comes from one hard object, like a peach pit, a chicken bone or a twist tie wrapped around the impeller. We free the flywheel from below with the hex key and pull the object with pliers, never a hand, then run it with water to make sure nothing else is stuck. The reset button may need a press. Repeat jams point us to the trap arm.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Artesia

    Humming with no spin almost always means a jam. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.
