    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Portales, NM

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Portales, NM

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. One cut, one fix. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Portales

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Portales home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Portales

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Portales home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Portales

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. The new pipe runs through the attic. For older Portales homes, it bypasses the whole buried run of tired copper or galvanized and removes the chance of another leak there. We keep drywall cuts small at fixture walls.

    Thermal and Acoustic Tools in Portales

    A thermal camera shows the warm streak a hot water leak leaves on the floor. Cold lines show no heat. On cold lines we rely on an acoustic sensor that listens through the slab, marking the point where the sound is loudest. Both tools ride on every Portales truck.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Portales

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. New PEX runs through the attic and down the walls while the old pipe stays buried, with openings placed behind vanities or in closets.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Portales

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Portales homes with original plumbing see this often.
