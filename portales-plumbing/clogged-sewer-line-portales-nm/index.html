    Main Line vs. Branch Line Clogs in Portales, NM

    Understanding the difference between a main sewer line clog and a branch line clog is key for proper diagnosis and repair in Portales, NM. A main line clog affects all drains in your home, often causing backups in lower-level fixtures. Branch line clogs are typically isolated to a single fixture or area. Our technicians are skilled at pinpointing the exact location of the blockage, whether it is in the main line or a specific branch, for a targeted and efficient repair.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Portales, NM

    Expansive clay soils in some areas swell and shrink with moisture, which can shift slab foundations and the pipes that run beneath them. Soil that swells, shrinks or settles can cause sections of sewer pipe to sink and form a belly. Waste and water collect in the low spot and lead to repeat clogs. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore proper slope.

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Portales

    Roots from thirsty trees and shrubs seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints.
