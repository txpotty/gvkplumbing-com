    PEX Water Line Replacement in Portales

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and budget-friendly choice for water line replacement in Portales. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for simpler installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with aggressive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in Portales, NM

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail without warning. Many insurers flag it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with modern materials. This reduces the chance of sudden leaks.
