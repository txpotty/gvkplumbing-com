    Key signs include very slow drains, gurgling sounds coming from drains or toilets, foul odors from drains, and water backing up in sinks, tubs, or toilets. If multiple fixtures are draining slowly, it often points to a main sewer line blockage requiring professional rooter service.

    Snaking uses an auger to break through and pull out blockages, while hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the entire inside of the pipe, removing buildup like grease and mineral deposits. Hydro-jetting provides a much more thorough cleaning, often recommended for recurring clogs or main sewer lines.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe walls. Many tough lines do best with both.

    Rooting cuts roots back but does not seal the pipe joints, so roots eventually regrow. Root treatments slow regrowth, and pipe lining or replacement provides a permanent fix.
