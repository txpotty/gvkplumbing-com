    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Portales

    To prevent future drain problems, avoid pouring grease down sinks, use drain screens to catch hair and food particles and be mindful of what goes down your toilets.     Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more snaking.
