    Rheem Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Portales

    Rheem's RTEX line covers most of the whole-home electric tankless units we install. The RTEX-18 runs on two 40 amp breakers, while the RTEX-24 and RTEX-27 need three, so we match the model to your panel before ordering. Wall mounting is simple. Water, temperature and leak checks finish the job.

    Residential Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Portales

    Residential electric tankless installs start with counting fixtures that run at the same time. Two showers at 2 gallons per minute each with 50 degree incoming water need roughly a 27 kW unit, and that unit needs three 40 amp double-pole breakers. Smaller homes need less. Our usual brands are Rheem, EcoSmart and Stiebel Eltron.

    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless Electric in Portales

    Switching from tank to tankless electric usually takes panel work and some re-piping. The higher amperage often means new breakers, heavier wire and sometimes a service upgrade, and the water lines get moved to meet the unit on the wall. We coordinate the electrician. You get floor space back.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Portales

    Hard water builds scale on tankless heating elements, which cuts efficiency and can trigger error codes. Many rural homes rely on private wells, and some groundwater carries naturally occurring arsenic or high mineral content that requires testing and treatment. We recommend a softener or a scale inhibitor ahead of the unit, and every install gets service valves so the heater can be flushed with a descaling solution once a year. Flushing takes an hour.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Portales, NM

    Upgrading from a 50 gallon electric tank to tankless is mostly an electrical project. Where the tank used one 30 amp circuit, a whole-home tankless often needs two or three 40 amp circuits. Plumbing is the easy part. Old tank removal is included.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Sizing and Selection in Portales

    Sizing comes down to two numbers, flow rate and temperature rise. If your groundwater comes in at 50 degrees and you want 120 at the shower, that is a 70 degree rise, and a 27 kW unit delivers about 2.6 gallons per minute at that rise. Summer water helps. Panel and pipe layout matter as well.

    Commercial Electric Tankless Water Heater Solutions in Portales, NM

    Salons, offices and small restaurants in Portales, NM use electric tankless to save space and cut standby loss. We install heavy-duty three-phase units where the building has that service, or several single-phase units at the points of use when it does not. We keep downtime short. We schedule around your business.

    Cost Comparison: Electric Tankless vs. Traditional in Portales, NM

    Upfront, electric tankless usually costs more than a tank because of the electrical work. A tankless can outlive two tanks and skips standby loss, which narrows the gap over time. Usage decides it. You get a real comparison first.
