    No Hot Water Service in Portales, NM

    Check the breaker first. Electric tanks often just need the breaker reset or the red high-limit button pushed. On gas units we work through the pilot, then the thermocouple, then the gas valve. Most calls end in under two hours.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Portales

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. It coats the heat exchanger and trips the unit into an error code. We descale with a pump and approved solution, clean the inlet filter and check the igniter and flow sensor while the unit is open. All major brands.

    Pilot Light Repair in Portales

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Less often it is a dirty flame arrestor screen choking off combustion air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Water Heater Maintenance and Tune-Ups in Portales, NM

    A yearly tune-up adds years to a tank. We flush the sediment, check the anode rod, test the relief valve and look over the gas or electrical connections.
