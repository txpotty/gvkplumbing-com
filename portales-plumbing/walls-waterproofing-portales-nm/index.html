    Drainage System Integration in Portales, NM

    Effective walls waterproofing often requires a solid drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. Our team designs and installs custom drainage solutions for Portales, NM properties, so water is efficiently managed and moved away from your home's vulnerable areas.

    Basement Wall Sealing in Portales, NM

    Basement wall sealing is a key part of walls waterproofing. Dry air and intense sun are the main climate stress in New Mexico, drying out rubber seals and breaking down exposed PVC and hose bibs. We apply high-quality sealants to porous concrete and masonry walls to prevent moisture from wicking through and causing dampness, mold, or musty odors. This service can be performed alone or as part of a larger waterproofing system, offering a drier and healthier environment in your Portales, NM basement.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Portales, NM

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Portales, NM

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This typically involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Portales

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, preventing mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. Dry air and intense sun are the main climate stress in New Mexico, drying out rubber seals and breaking down exposed PVC and hose bibs. An encapsulated crawl space can significantly improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home.     Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Portales

    Even minor cracks in foundation walls can allow serious water intrusion. Our services include crack repair using epoxy or polyurethane injection to seal these entry points for good. This method fills the entire depth of the crack, restoring strength and preventing water penetration. We address both vertical and horizontal cracks, knowing that horizontal cracks can indicate structural issues that need a thorough assessment before sealing. Addressing these early prevents further water damage and expensive future repairs.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Portales, NM

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves.     Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Portales

It's an affordable way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.
