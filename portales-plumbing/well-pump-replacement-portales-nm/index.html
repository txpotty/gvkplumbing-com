    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Portales

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. On the way up, we look at the torque arrestor and the splices. Figure on one day.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Portales

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. Short cycling, where the pump starts every few seconds of water use, usually means the tank bladder has failed and the pump is paying for it. We check the air charge with a gauge. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Portales

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Sand in the impellers and lightning surges are the usual culprits. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. That tells us whether the problem is down the hole or up top.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Portales

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. Wire gets the same look. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Portales

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. We replace those pumps and the starters that protect them. We schedule around watering times.

    Well Pump Sizing in Portales

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Portales, NM

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. Air sneaks in through a pinhole in the suction line, the pump loses prime and the seal burns up running dry. We pressure test the line. If the well is too deep for a jet pump, we will say so and quote a submersible.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Portales, NM

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. Pressure switches with burned contacts or a clogged pickup tube can leave the pump sitting idle while the tank drains to zero. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. We show you the burned contacts.
