    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Portales, NM

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. O-rings and fittings get replaced and pressure tested. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Control Valve Troubleshooting and Repair in Portales, NM

    Stuck pistons, worn seals and failed drive motors cause most control valve trouble, and the symptoms are hard water, constant regeneration or a leak at the head. The valve gets tested in place. Our plumbers carry seal kits and pistons for Fleck, Clack and Autotrol heads, which covers a big share of the softeners installed in homes around here.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Portales

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Portales

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Bed Problems in Portales

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. New resin restores full capacity.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Portales, NM

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Fill the tank halfway.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Portales, NM

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Portales

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. GVK Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.
