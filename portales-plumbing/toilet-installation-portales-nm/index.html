    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Portales, NM

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Portales

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Portales

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Portales, NM

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Portales, NM

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Think about who will use it before you buy. Shorter adults and kids sometimes find the taller seat leaves their feet dangling, while anyone with bad knees or a hip replacement usually finds it much easier to sit and stand. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Portales

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Portales, NM

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Portales

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Portales

    Labor for a standard toilet installation in Portales typically falls between $250 and $600, not counting the toilet itself. Flange repairs add to that. A rotted flange or a soft subfloor can push the total higher, and we price that work before it starts instead of surprising you on the invoice.

    Lower water bills come first. Pre-1994 toilets can use nearly three times what a 1.28 gallon model does. Newer bowls also have wider glazed trapways that clog less, and the smoother porcelain stays cleaner between scrubbings than an old pitted bowl ever did.

    Watch the base and the tank. A puddle that returns after wiping means a crack or a bad seal. We rule out the seal first because it is cheaper, but cracked porcelain cannot be fixed for good, so a crack means a new toilet.

    Homeowners set basic toilets all the time. The trouble spots are flange height and bolt torque. A flange that sits below the finished floor needs an extender or a thicker seal, and a nut tightened one turn too far can crack the base, so on new floors and wall-hung toilets a pro install is the safer bet.

    For a replacement in the same spot, usually no. Additions and new baths normally need one. Requirements differ by jurisdiction, so we call the building department first and pull the permit when the job needs it. That cost goes on the quote.

    We guarantee the workmanship on every toilet we set. Any leak from our install is our problem. That covers the seal and the supply connection, while manufacturing defects in the toilet fall under the maker's warranty, which we help you file if a part fails early. Hang on to your receipt.

    Cracks in the tank or bowl are the clearest sign. Some cracks are hard to see at first. Look for a slow drip on the floor that comes back after you dry it, or frequent clogs on an early low-flow model with a narrow trapway. So is a toilet you have rebuilt twice in three years.
