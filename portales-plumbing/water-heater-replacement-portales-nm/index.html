    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Portales, NM

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Portales, NM homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. Gas gets shut off and capped, the old tank drained and removed, and the new one set with a fresh connector, sediment trap and properly sloped vent. Every joint gets checked.
