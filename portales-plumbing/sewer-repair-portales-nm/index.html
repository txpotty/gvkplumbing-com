    Root Intrusion Repair in Portales, NM

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups.     Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Portales

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease and wipes cause most of it.     Pipe Burst and Collapse Repair in Portales, NM

    A collapsed sewer pipe stops all flow at the point of failure. The whole house loses its drains. Collapses usually come from corroded cast iron, crushed clay under a driveway or tar-paper pipe that has finally flattened, and the camera tells us exactly where the pipe gave way and how deep it sits. We dig at the mark and replace the broken length with PVC. Lining cannot fix a full collapse.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Portales, NM

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.
