    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in Alamogordo

    Pricing depends on the job. Simple repairs like a faucet cartridge or toilet rebuild tend to cost $150 to $350, a water heater installation is often $1,200 to $3,500, and slab leak repairs or full repipes can reach several thousand dollars. The exact number comes after the diagnosis.

    Once a year is a good rule. Hard water speeds up sediment buildup, so homes with scale may need a flush more often, and a yearly check lets us test the relief valve, look at the anode rod and catch a weeping fitting before it rusts through the jacket. Anodes usually last three to five years.

    Our plumbers install and service Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Rheem, Bradford White and Navien, among others. We will tell you which ones hold up in your water, since cartridges in some brands handle hard water better than others. Some cost more but last longer.

    Every one to two years works for most homes in Alamogordo, NM. A visit takes about an hour, and we check pressure, look for slow leaks at shutoffs and supply lines, test the water heater relief valve and look for corrosion on exposed pipe. Problems found early stay cheap.

    A slab leak is a break in a water line running under a concrete foundation. Caliche, a hard, cement-like soil layer, is common in New Mexico and can make digging for sewer and water line repairs difficult work. Warm spots on the floor, a sudden jump in the water bill or the sound of water running with every fixture off are the usual signs. Do not wait on it.

    Start at the water meter. Shut off every tap and appliance, then watch the small leak dial, and if it still spins, look for warm spots on the floor, cracks in tile, mildew smell or a water bill that jumped without explanation. A pressure test confirms it.

    Gurgling is the early one. Slow drains, sewer smells and water backing up in a shower are the others, and when more than one fixture acts up at the same time, the clog is usually in the main line rather than a single trap. Main line clogs rarely clear on their own.

    Every repair comes with a written parts and labor warranty. We also register the manufacturer warranty on new fixtures and heaters for you, which matters because some water heater makers shorten the tank warranty if the unit is never registered. Keep a photo of the serial plate.
