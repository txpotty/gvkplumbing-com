    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Alamogordo, NM

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Caliche, a hard, cement-like soil layer, is common in New Mexico and can make digging for sewer and water line repairs slow work. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel.     Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Alamogordo

    Trap and slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Replacing pipe costs more.     Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair changes the pipe itself. We check on video.

    For a fixture unclog or a trap swap, usually not. Big replacements are different.     Repeat clogs come from something in the pipe catching debris. Grease or scale is common. Poor pipe slope, a missing vent or a cracked fitting with a rough edge will snag paper and hair until the line closes off again a few weeks after each cleaning. We find it on video.

    Slow drains, gurgling and sewer smell are the big three warnings. So is water under the sink. A drain that clogs again a few weeks after it was cleared, or water spots below a bathroom, point to a pipe problem instead of a simple clog. Catch it early.

    Hydro-jetting scours the pipe with water at several thousand psi. The whole inside gets cleaned. A snake only punches a hole through a clog, while a jetter removes grease, scale and fine roots from the full circumference of the pipe. Fragile pipe gets checked before jetting.
