    Over time, mineral deposits and other materials can build up on the interior walls of sewer lines, forming a tough scale. This reduces pipe diameter and can lead to repeat clogs. Hydro jetting is the most effective method for sewer line descaling, using powerful water jets to break down and flush away these mineral deposits, restoring the pipe's full carrying capacity and slowing future buildup.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Lovington

    Residential hydro jetting in Lovington usually costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and flush debris away.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection reveals damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have gotten into your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.

    While hydro jetting fully removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can go over during our assessment.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more complete and longer-lasting clean.

    A standard residential hydro jetting service in Lovington usually takes between 1 and 3 hours. This includes the initial camera inspection, the jetting process itself, and a final inspection. More extensive commercial jobs or severe main line blockages might require more time.
