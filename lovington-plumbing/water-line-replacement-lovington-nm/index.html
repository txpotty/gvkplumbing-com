    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Lovington, NM

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper.     Type L vs. Type K Copper in Lovington, NM

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against stone contact and soil chemistry. Same fittings, different pipe.

    PEX Water Line Installation in Lovington, NM

    PEX bends around corners, so a typical service run needs no fittings between the meter and the house.
