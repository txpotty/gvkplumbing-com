    Sump Pump Types We Install in Lovington, NM

    Wayne Sump Pump Replacement in Lovington

    Wayne makes everything from a thermoplastic quarter-horse pump at the hardware store to cast iron models with vertical switches, and the cheap ones are what we replace most. Most dead Wayne pumps we see have a cooked motor or a float tangled in the power cord. You get a matching Wayne or a sturdier cast iron model, your call. Takes about two hours.

    Storm-Night Sump Pump Replacement in Lovington, NM

    Most emergency sump calls come in at night, during the heaviest rain of the year, when the pump has been running nonstop for hours. Those calls go to the top of the schedule. The tech brings a replacement pump and a new check valve and can have water moving out of the pit within an hour of arrival in most cases. Then we find out why it failed.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Lovington, NM

    Yard drainage pits and window well pumps can sit dry from fall to spring and then seize the first time a big storm fills them. We clean the debris out and install a pump built to handle grit. Discharge goes away from the foundation. A bucket test every spring catches problems early.

    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Lovington, NM

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They are handy when the basin is too small for two pumps side by side. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    High-Capacity Sump Pump Upgrades in Lovington

    If your pump runs every 30 seconds during a storm and still loses ground, it is undersized for the water coming in. First we time the pump cycles and measure the pit refill rate. From there it is math. Often a 1/2 or 3/4 HP pump with more GPM at your lift, plus a 2-inch discharge line, solves it.

    Light Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Lovington, NM

    Light commercial work, think retail basements and small office buildings, usually means a 1/2 HP or larger cast iron pump with a 2-inch discharge and a panel alarm. We replace like for like or upsize. Grinder and ejector pumps are a separate job.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Lovington

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. When the check valve leaks, the water in the pipe drops back into the basin and the pump has to move it twice, which shortens its life. New valve every time. Vertical switches suit tight pits, and tethered floats suit wide ones.

    Cast Iron Submersible Pumps in Lovington

    Cast iron sheds motor heat into the water around it far better than a plastic housing does, which is a big part of why a Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 outlasts a hardware-store pump. Figure on an hour or two for a typical swap. Before we set it, we make sure the float can swing freely without catching the wall.

    Local Sump Pump Replacement in Lovington, NM

    Local drainage matters. We check the yard grade and the discharge outlet, since water that drains back toward the foundation just gets pumped again and again. Sometimes the fix is ten feet of pipe.

    Search for a sump pump plumber near you in Lovington, NM, and you will find plenty of names, but fewer that stock the pump on the truck. Most of our trucks carry cast iron 1/3 and 1/2 HP pumps. That means a same-day swap in most cases.
