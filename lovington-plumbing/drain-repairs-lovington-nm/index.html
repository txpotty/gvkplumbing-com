    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Lovington

    The cost of drain repairs in Lovington, NM can vary a lot, typically ranging from $150 for a simple clog removal to over $3,000 for complex main sewer line repairs. Factors like the type of drain, severity of the issue and access to the pipes all affect the final price. We provide a detailed estimate after a thorough inspection.

    Drain lines can break or collapse due to several factors, including shifting soil, tree root intrusion, corrosion from age, extreme temperature changes or heavy loads passing over underground pipes. Poor installation or defective materials can also contribute to early failure.

    Minor drain repairs, such as clearing a simple clog, typically do not require a permit in Lovington. However, significant repairs involving altering or replacing major sections of your plumbing system, especially main sewer lines, may require permits. GVK Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications for such projects.

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful drain cleaning method that uses high-pressure water to clear out stubborn blockages, grease buildup, mineral deposits and even tree roots from your pipes. It is often used for severe clogs or as a preventive measure to thoroughly clean drain lines, leaving them almost like new.

    Drain cleaning removes a blockage from an otherwise sound pipe. Drain repair fixes the pipe itself, such as a crack, bad slope, broken fitting or missing vent. Many recurring clogs need repair, not more snaking.

    The duration of drain repairs depends entirely on the issue. A simple clog can often be cleared in an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of a main sewer line, might take a full day or even several days, especially if permits or major digging are involved.

    Minor clogs can sometimes be resolved with plungers or enzymatic drain cleaners. However, for stubborn blockages, recurring issues or suspected pipe damage, it is best to call a professional. DIY attempts with harsh chemical cleaners can sometimes damage pipes or hide a larger problem.

    Common signs include slow draining sinks or tubs, gurgling noises from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your drains, recurring clogs or water backing up into fixtures. If you notice any of these, it is wise to call a professional before the problem worsens.
