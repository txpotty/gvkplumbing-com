    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Lovington, NM

    Many modern single-handle faucets, often found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Lovington

    A dripping spout usually means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from adding up on your water bill.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair in Lovington

    Kitchen faucets endure heavy daily use and are prone to specific issues like sprayer malfunctions, clogged aerators, or swivel problems. Our plumbers are adept at repairing all kitchen faucet styles, including pull-down, pull-out, and commercial-style fixtures. We understand the importance of a fully functional kitchen sink for cooking and cleaning. We address everything from leaks around the base to issues with water filtration attachments.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Lovington, NM

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement might be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can reduce your repair cost.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Lovington, NM

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water damage.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Lovington

    Compression faucets, often found in older Lovington homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's usually due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks for good. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Lovington, NM

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's probably time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are typically the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Lovington

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a common choice in many contemporary Lovington bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.
