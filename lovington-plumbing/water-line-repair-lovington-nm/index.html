    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Lovington, NM

    Galvanized steel service lines rust from the inside out. By the time one leaks, the rest of the pipe is close behind.     Main Water Line Leak Repair in Lovington

    A meter that moves with every fixture off is the clearest sign you have. We confirm it in about ten minutes.     PEX Water Line Repair in Lovington, NM

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Lovington

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Housing in New Mexico ranges from historic adobe homes to newer stucco subdivisions, and plumbing materials vary just as widely. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Water Line Repair at the Meter in Lovington, NM

    The meter box is where a lot of leaks start. Compression fittings there work loose over time. Before we touch anything, we figure out whether the leak is before or after the meter, because the water provider owns the meter and everything on the street side. When it is on your side, we repair it. That often means new fittings and a new customer shutoff valve on the same visit.

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Lovington, NM

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Water softeners and filtration systems are popular around Lovington because they extend the life of fixtures and cut down on spotting. Scale builds up inside and the opening shrinks. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, and so is pressure that falls off when the shower and the washer run together. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Most homes are done in a day.

    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Lovington

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. With PEX, the fitting is the weak point most of the time. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Lovington

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, while new HDPE comes through right behind the head. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.
