    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Lovington

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Lovington, NM

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Lovington, NM

    Evaporative coolers are common here, and their supply lines, float valves and drain lines need seasonal maintenance to avoid leaks.
