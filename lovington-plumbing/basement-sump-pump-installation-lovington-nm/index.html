    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Lovington

    Outages and heavy rain tend to show up together. A battery backup keeps a second pump running without house power, and a fully charged 75 amp-hour battery can run a typical backup pump for several hours of on-and-off cycling, longer on a light night. We load test the battery.

    Sump Pit Installation in Lovington, NM

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. A small 15 inch pit makes the pump cycle every minute in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Lovington

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. A spring check valve stops the column from falling back into the pit, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Freeze risk in New Mexico depends heavily on elevation, with northern and mountain areas seeing hard freezes every winter and lower desert areas seeing fewer. Where lines can freeze, we fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    Some lots sit in a high water table and fill a standard pit every few minutes during spring. For those, we step up to a 1/2 or 3/4 horsepower unit in a bigger basin, all sized after we time how fast your pit refills. Timing the refill comes first.
