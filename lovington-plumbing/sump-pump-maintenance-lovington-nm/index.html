    Sump Pump Maintenance in Lovington, NM: Pits, Floats and Valves

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Lovington, NM

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. We check that intake and the float mechanism for buildup. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Lovington, NM

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Float Switch Checks in Lovington, NM

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Lovington

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. We pump the basin down and scoop out the sediment by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Lovington

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. We also clean the intake screen and check the housing for corrosion, which shows up fast on pumps that sit in standing water for years.

    Commercial Building Sump Pumps in Lovington, NM

    Parking garages, mechanical rooms and loading docks often drain to large pits with pumps rated at a horsepower or more. They need checking more often than anything in a house. Our crew tests the controls, the alarm panel and each pump in a duplex station, and records amp draw every visit so a slow decline gets caught. Downtime costs money.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Lovington, NM

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. That is the moment a backup pump earns its keep. Our plumber reads the battery date, cleans corrosion off the terminals and tests the charger, because a failed charger can leave the battery flat within a season.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Lovington

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.
