    Drain Line Repiping in Lovington

    While often overlooked, your home's drain lines are just as important as your supply lines. Old cast iron or corroded drain pipes can lead to frequent clogs, slow drainage, and even sewage backups. GVK Plumbing provides complete drain line repiping in Lovington, replacing failing waste pipes with new, smooth PVC or ABS lines. This supports efficient waste removal and prevents future drainage problems, protecting your home from foul odors and unsanitary conditions.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Lovington

    Copper piping offers excellent durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Lovington. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including changing temperatures and local water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Lovington

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.
