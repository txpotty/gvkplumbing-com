    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Santa Fe, NM

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts.     High-Efficiency Toilet (HET) Upgrades in Santa Fe, NM

    High-efficiency toilets carry a WaterSense label and a MaP score, which tells you how many grams of waste the toilet clears in one flush. Look for a score of at least 800. A high score means fewer double flushes and fewer clogs, and that is where the real water savings come from.

    Toilet Replacement Cost Comparison in Santa Fe, NM

    Three repair calls in a year on the same toilet usually cost more than replacing it, especially when the toilet is old enough to waste water on every flush. You get repair and replacement prices next to each other. The decision is yours, and we do not push replacement on a toilet that only needs a flapper.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Santa Fe

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. You should replace it. We pull the cracked toilet and check the floor for water damage before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Santa Fe, NM

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are noticeably louder than gravity toilets. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Santa Fe

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Santa Fe

    Going from a standard bowl to a comfort-height model raises the seat two or three inches, which matters a lot for seniors and anyone recovering from knee or hip surgery. No drain changes are needed. If there is room, a grab bar beside it helps too, and we can anchor that into a stud during the same visit.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Santa Fe, NM

    Office and retail restrooms wear out toilets faster than any house, and a replacement there usually means a floor-mount or wall-hung bowl with a flushometer instead of a tank. We schedule around your hours. We match the bowl to the gallons per flush on the valve, since a mismatch leaves waste behind or wastes water.

    It is doable for handy homeowners, but old toilets are heavy and flange problems are common once the bowl comes off. A pro install avoids cracked china and slow leaks.

    A handle that needs jiggling, a tank that refills on its own, a hiss after the flush or a flush that stops halfway all point to worn tank parts. Those can be replaced one at a time. On a toilet past 25 years, replacing the whole fixture often makes more sense.

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drain, which you can find by measuring to the closet bolts. Twelve inches is standard.
