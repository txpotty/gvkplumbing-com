    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Santa Fe

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Leak Detection and Assessment in Santa Fe, NM

    Hidden leaks waste water and quietly damage floors and walls. Start at the meter.     Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Santa Fe

    Most home plumbing inspections run $150 to $350, depending on the size and age of the house. Camera work costs extra. Adding a sewer camera inspection raises the total, and we tell you the full price before the visit so there is nothing new on the invoice when we finish.

    We check water supply lines, drain lines, fixtures, the water heater, sump pump, garbage disposal and exposed gas lines. We test how drains flow. We also look for leaks, corrosion and venting problems, read the water pressure and write up the condition of each part so you know where the system stands.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Gurgling drains count too. Damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell under a sink or a warm patch of tile can all point to a hidden leak that an inspection will find before it does more damage.

    A one to two year routine works for most homes. Older homes and properties with past leaks may benefit from yearly inspections.
