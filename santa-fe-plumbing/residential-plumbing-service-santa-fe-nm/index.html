    Two leaks in a year is the tipping point for most homes. Polybutylene or galvanized supply pipe pushes it toward a repipe, too, since patching one spot on a failing system just moves the next leak a few feet down the line. We price both side by side.

    Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. Soft water is easier on fixtures.

    Soft water and normal pressure prevent a lot of what we see. Grease out of the drains and a yearly pressure test help too, since anything over 80 psi wears out supply hoses and fixtures faster than people expect. A yearly inspection catches the rest.
