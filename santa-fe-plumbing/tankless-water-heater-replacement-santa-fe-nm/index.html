    Common signs that your tankless water heater needs replacement include frequent error codes, inconsistent water temperature, reduced hot water flow or a complete lack of hot water.     Well-maintained tankless units can last 15 to 20 years. In hard water areas without regular descaling, heat exchangers can fail far sooner. Annual flushing makes the largest difference.
