    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydrojet Plumbing in Santa Fe

    The cost of hydrojet plumbing in Santa Fe, NM can vary, usually ranging from $300 to $800, depending on the severity and location of the clog, as well as the length and diameter of the pipe. Complex jobs or main sewer line blockages may cost more, sometimes up to $1,500. We provide a clear estimate after an initial assessment.

    When performed by a trained professional, hydrojetting is generally safe for most types of pipes, including older cast iron or PVC. However, severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes might not withstand the high pressure. This is why GVK Plumbing always recommends a pre-service camera inspection to check pipe integrity before proceeding with hydrojet plumbing.

    Repeat clogs, slow drains throughout the house, gurgling sounds and sewer odors all suggest buildup in the line. Kitchens that back up every few months are a classic sign of grease. A camera inspection confirms whether jetting is the right fix.

    We use professional-grade hydrojetting equipment from reputable manufacturers like Spartan Tool, US Jetting, and Gorlitz. These brands are known for their reliability, power, and effectiveness in clearing even the toughest pipe blockages, ensuring a thorough clean every time.

    Most residential jobs take one to three hours, including camera inspections. Commercial lines with multiple runs can take a half day or more. We give you an estimate at booking.

    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, often leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing buildup and leaving it clean. Hydrojetting provides a much more comprehensive and longer-lasting solution.

    Hydrojetting is generally safe for most modern plumbing pipes, including PVC, ABS, and cast iron, when performed by a skilled professional. However, it may not be suitable for severely corroded, cracked, or improperly installed pipes. We always perform a pre-inspection to confirm pipe integrity.

    Usually, a permit is not required for standard hydrojet plumbing services that involve clearing existing drain or sewer lines within a private property in Santa Fe.
