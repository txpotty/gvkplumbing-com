    Missing outlet tees are the single most common repair we make. They get knocked loose during pumping more than you would think. Without the tee, the outlet draws from the top of the tank, right where the grease floats, and the trap sends grease to the sewer while looking perfectly fine from above. A new tee takes about thirty minutes.

    Experienced Team

    Diagnosing a grease trap is mostly about knowing what normal looks like. In a working trap, grease floats in a layer on top and the middle stays fairly clear. Our techs measure both layers, compare them to the last pumping date, and can tell from that whether the trap is separating or just passing grease along to the sewer. The numbers tell the story.

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Santa Fe

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean.
