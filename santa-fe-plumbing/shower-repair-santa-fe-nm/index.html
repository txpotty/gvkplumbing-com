    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. A worn spout gate or a tired diverter stem lets water keep running from the spout, and the head pressure drops off. We replace the spout or stem. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Santa Fe, NM

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them.     Weak Shower Spray Repair in Santa Fe, NM

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Scale packs the nozzles and the restrictor screen behind them, and the spray drops off a little at a time until one day it feels like a garden mister. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Showerhead Repair and Replacement in Santa Fe

    Showerheads clog from the inside out. Minerals collect behind the face and inside the restrictor, which turns the spray patchy or weak with the valve fully open. A vinegar soak can work. Otherwise we put on a new head with rubber nozzles that rub clean with your thumb.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Santa Fe

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Santa Fe
