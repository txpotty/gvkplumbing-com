    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Santa Fe, NM

    Type L Copper Repiping in Santa Fe

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. Our copper repipes use Type L with proper hangers and isolators so the pipe does not wear against framing. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Commercial Repipe Services in Santa Fe

    Older commercial buildings in Santa Fe often have galvanized risers, copper that has pinholed or plastic pipe that no longer meets code. Our crews swap out risers, branches and drains with as little interruption to tenants as the building allows. Shutoffs get scheduled ahead. Work is phased by zone, and we coordinate access, keys and noise limits with the property manager.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Santa Fe, NM

    Polybutylene, a gray or blue plastic pipe installed in many homes from the late 1970s into the mid 1990s, breaks down with exposure to chlorine and other disinfectants in tap water. It fails without warning. Brittle pipe walls and cracked fittings mean one polybutylene leak usually signals risk across the whole house. Full replacement is the fix. Many insurers ask about it.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Santa Fe

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Residential Repiping Plans in Santa Fe

    Every house is piped a little differently, so a repipe starts with a walk-through. Our estimator counts fixtures, checks access, finds the main and asks about any planned remodels before choosing a route. Then we size the lines. A three-bath house with a tankless heater may need 1-inch main trunk lines to keep showers steady when the washer kicks on.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Santa Fe

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Santa Fe

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. Our office works with your HOA on approvals and shutoff times, and we take steps to protect the units next door. Building-wide jobs go in phases. For larger buildings, we repipe one stack or wing at a time so residents can stay in place.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Santa Fe

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it. We stop the active leak first, make temporary repairs that get water back on and then plan the full repipe so you are not rushed into it. That gives you time. Your policy may pay for water damage even if not for the new pipe, so we take photos of everything we find.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Santa Fe

    Most PEX repipes for New Mexico homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size, bathrooms and access. Copper typically adds 20 to 40 percent.

    Most repipes in Santa Fe, NM take three to five working days for an average house. Larger homes run longer. Copper adds time because each turn needs a fitting, and patching usually takes one more day after the plumbing passes.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A with ProPEX fittings and Type L copper from domestic mills. We also use Viega ProPress fittings for copper work.

    A whole-home repipe requires permits, pressure testing and code knowledge. A licensed crew usually finishes faster and passes inspection the first time.

    You get a written workmanship warranty covering labor and materials. Details are spelled out up front. Our repipes use Uponor PEX-A or domestic Type L copper, and certified PEX-A jobs also get the manufacturer's long-term warranty.

    Most homes are repiped in two to four days, plus a day for drywall patching. You will have water most nights.

    Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale accumulates quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. Hard water deposits calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe over time. Pressure falls as scale builds. Scale also speeds corrosion in metal lines, which is why PEX-A plus a softener or filter added during a repipe can protect the new system and your fixtures.

    We have to open some drywall, but the openings are small and planned. Most are about the size of a sheet of paper. We cut clean squares at fixtures and along the route, patch every one after the inspection and match the existing texture, so the walls are ready to paint when we finish.
