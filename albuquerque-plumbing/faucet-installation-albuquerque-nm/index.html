    Brand-Specific Faucet Installation in Albuquerque

    Every brand mounts a little differently. Moen and Delta each use their own quick-connect hose fitting, and Kohler often ships a different mounting bracket. Following the maker's sheet step by step keeps the warranty good. We put in Grohe and Brizo faucets most weeks as well.

    Garage and Workshop Faucets in Albuquerque

    Workshop and garage sinks get abused. We install faucets with metal handles and a threaded spout, anchored to the wall or the tub deck so a pulled hose does not twist the supply lines. We check the flow at full open. If the tub drains slow, we clear the trap while we are there.

    Commercial and ADA Faucets in Albuquerque, NM

    ADA rules set faucet reach and handle force. Handles have to work with one hand and no tight grip, which is why lever and sensor faucets dominate commercial restrooms. We schedule around your hours. We leave the spec sheets on site for the inspector.

    Restaurant and Office Faucets in Albuquerque, NM

    Restaurant pre-rinse units mount to the wall and the sink deck. We bolt the wall bracket into solid backing and fit check valves on both supplies, since a pre-rinse unit gets pulled hard all shift. Hand-wash sinks get wrist blades. Night installs keep the kitchen open.

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Albuquerque

    Touchless faucets run on a solenoid valve. We mount the control box high on the cabinet wall, away from where water collects, and power it with batteries or a plug-in adapter. Mineral scale can stick a solenoid open, so we add the inline strainer the maker supplies. Sensor range gets tested last.

    Vessel Sink Faucets in Albuquerque, NM

    Vessel sinks sit high, so the faucet has to sit higher. We use either a tall deck faucet or a wall spout, sized so water hits near the drain and not the rim where it splashes onto the counter. Reach matters as much as height. Too short and you bang your knuckles on the bowl.

    Kitchen Faucet Installation in Albuquerque

    Pull-down sprayers get yanked a thousand times a year. That is why we clip the hose weight at the right spot on the hose and check that the hose does not snag on the shutoffs or the disposal when it pulls back, which is where most early kitchen leaks start. Both stops get new braided stainless supply lines. Last thing, we run hot and cold for a few minutes and wipe the cabinet floor with a dry towel to look for any drip.

    Replacing a Leaky Faucet in Albuquerque, NM

    A faucet dripping once a second wastes a few thousand gallons a year. If the valve body is pitted or no one makes the cartridge anymore, a new faucet costs less than chasing the drip again. We pull the old one. The new faucet goes on with fresh supplies and gets a full leak check, usually inside an hour.
