    Tankless Error Code Diagnostics in Albuquerque, NM

    Every tankless brand flashes its own codes, so a code 11 on one unit means something else on another. We read the code, pull the history and test the part tied to it. Guessing gets expensive.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in Albuquerque

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling Service in Albuquerque

    Scale is calcium and magnesium carbonate that comes out of hard water as it heats up, and a tankless exchanger heats water fast through narrow passages.     Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Albuquerque, NM

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. A breaker that trips under load can mean a shorted element or a loose lug that has been arcing, and both get tested with a meter before anything is replaced. Element resistance and supply voltage get measured. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Albuquerque

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, then trim the burner using thermistor readings. A dirty inlet screen or a failing sensor shows up as no hot water, temperature swings or a unit that drops out mid-shower. We pull the screen, read sensor values in the service menu and compare thermistor resistance to the chart. Usually under an hour.

    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Albuquerque

We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, run descaler through the exchanger for about an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.
