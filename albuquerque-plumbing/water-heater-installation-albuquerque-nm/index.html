    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system.     Electric Water Heater Installation in Albuquerque, NM

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Albuquerque

    Most installs in Albuquerque, NM land between $1,200 and $3,500. Tank versus tankless, size, gas versus electric and any needed upgrades all move the number. Attics add labor. We give you the figure before work starts.

    Our crews install Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz and Navien. If you want to stick with your current brand, we can usually get it.

    Age, repair cost and efficiency decide it. If the heater is over ten years old or the repair costs more than half of a new one, replacing it usually makes more sense. A leaking tank is always a replacement.

    Rusty hot water, rumbling from the tank, water at the base and an age past ten years are the big ones. Running out of hot water faster than you used to usually means sediment has filled the bottom of the tank or a dip tube has broken. Any of those deserves a look.

    For two to four people, a 40 or 50 gallon tank usually does the job. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    Homes with a pressure reducing valve or check valve usually need one. It protects the heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    Tank heaters usually last 10 to 15 years, and tankless units can go 20 or more. Hard water and skipped maintenance shorten that, while yearly flushing and anode checks stretch it a good deal past the average. Flushing helps most.

    Manufacturer warranties on the tank and parts usually run anywhere from 6 to 12 years, depending on the model and the brand you choose. GVK Plumbing backs the installation labor too. We review terms with you.
