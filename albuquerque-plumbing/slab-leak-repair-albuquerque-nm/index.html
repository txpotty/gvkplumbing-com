    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Albuquerque, NM

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it a strong choice for many homes.     Slab Leak Reroute in Albuquerque

    Rerouting is often the best long-term choice, especially with older copper lines. We abandon the leaking line under the slab and run a new PEX line through the attic and walls. Openings are small and placed where patching is simplest. Reroutes eliminate future leaks in that section of pipe.

    Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Albuquerque, NM

    A large slab leak can push water up through flooring or into walls quickly. We help you shut off the water and send a technician to isolate the leaking line. Often we can restore water to the rest of the house while we plan the repair. Fast action limits flooring damage and soil saturation.

    Whether your home in Albuquerque, NM has modern PEX piping or traditional copper lines, our plumbers are skilled in repairing both.     Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Albuquerque

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an effective long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Albuquerque with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Albuquerque

    Cold water slab leaks are harder to feel but still waste water and wet the soil. They often show up as a high water bill or a meter that moves with all fixtures off. We isolate the cold line, locate the leak and repair or reroute it. Fixing cold leaks quickly helps protect your foundation.
