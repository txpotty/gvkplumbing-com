    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Albuquerque

    A standard plumbing inspection in Albuquerque, NM usually costs $150 to $350. Size changes the price. The base price covers pipe, fixtures, the heater, drains and pressure, and the sewer camera is usually a separate line since it adds time and equipment. We quote it on the phone.

    High water bills, repeat clogs, slow drains and discolored water are common signs. Banging pipes count too. Strange sounds from the pipes or the water heater, along with visible moisture or mold near fixtures, usually point to an underlying problem that needs a professional look before it gets worse.

    We check water supply lines, drain lines, fixtures, the water heater, sump pump, garbage disposal and exposed gas lines. Drainage gets tested.     We identify supply pipe materials including polybutylene, Kitec, galvanized, copper, CPVC and PEX. Known risks and options go in the report.

    A standard home inspection takes about one and a half to three hours. Sewer video adds time. Larger homes, long sewer lines or a hidden leak that needs tracing can push it longer, and we will tell you the expected time when you book so you can plan around it.

    Most homes do well with an inspection every one to two years. Before buying is a good time too. Buyers, owners of older houses and anyone with recent leaks or backups benefit from yearly checks that catch problems sooner.

    Our technicians inspect all major water heater brands, including Rheem, AO Smith and Bradford White. Tankless is included. We also check Rinnai and Noritz tankless heaters and the common fixture brands found in most homes, so the brand on your equipment will not limit what we can inspect.

    Looking for drips and watching the meter is worth doing on your own. It has limits.
