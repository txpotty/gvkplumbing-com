    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Albuquerque, NM

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Albuquerque, NM

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. Swapping a pump in an existing pit is the cheapest job, while a new pit means saw cutting, digging, gravel and a concrete patch, which roughly doubles the labor. Backups add cost. We list each option on a written quote.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Albuquerque

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Albuquerque, NM

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Albuquerque, NM

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Albuquerque, NM

    A dead pump during a storm means the water is already climbing. We carry common pumps on the truck. Most swaps take about two hours. We test it with a full pit before leaving.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Albuquerque, NM

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Albuquerque

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.
