    The cost for frozen pipe repair in Albuquerque can range from $200 for simple thawing to $1,500 or more for burst pipe repair requiring extensive access or slab work.     While no pipe is completely freeze-proof, materials like PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, are more flexible and less likely to suffer bursting when frozen compared to rigid copper or PVC. We also recommend insulating existing pipes with foam pipe sleeves from brands like Frost King or installing self-regulating heat cables from companies like EasyHeat for maximum protection.

    Disconnect hoses, cover outdoor faucets and backflow preventers, and let a faucet drip on exterior walls during hard freezes. Insulating attic, crawl space and garage lines helps most. We can install insulation, heat cable or reroute vulnerable pipes.

    While any pipe can freeze, older galvanized steel or copper pipes in uninsulated areas are often more susceptible to bursting due to their rigid material. PEX piping is typically more freeze-resistant because it can expand, though it can still freeze and burst under extreme conditions. Our team works with all pipe types.

    If your pipes are frozen but not burst, immediately turn off your main water supply to prevent flooding if the pipes do burst when thawing. Open the affected faucets to relieve pressure and then call GVK Plumbing at (505) 581-4975 for professional assistance. Avoid using open flames to thaw pipes.

    To prevent pipes from freezing in Albuquerque, insulate all exposed pipes, especially those in unheated areas like crawl spaces, attics, and basements. Disconnect garden hoses, drain outdoor spigots, and consider keeping a small trickle of water running from faucets during extreme cold snaps. Sealing air leaks near pipes can also make a difference.

    Many policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, though coverage varies. We can provide photos and a written report of the repair. Check with your insurer about your own policy.
