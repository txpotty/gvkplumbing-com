    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Albuquerque

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Rusty water is a sign. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, so we swap the failing pipe for PEX or copper with proper fittings between metals. Pressure comes right back.

    Emergency Pipe Repair in Albuquerque, NM

    Water coming through a ceiling is not a next-week problem. Our phones stay on.     Pinhole Leak Repair in Albuquerque

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water chemistry causes most. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. More pinholes often follow.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Albuquerque

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Flow drops slowly. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, which makes full replacement of the affected run the smarter spend. We use dielectric unions at transitions.

    Buried Line Repair in Albuquerque

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Freeze risk in New Mexico depends heavily on elevation, with northern and mountain areas seeing hard freezes every winter and lower desert areas seeing fewer. Service lines fail often. We locate the leak without guessing, then repair it through a small pit or replace the line with a trenchless pull when the old pipe is beyond patching. Drain lines get video first.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Albuquerque

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. Fittings let go. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, so we replace the failed joint with the right fitting for that brand of PEX. Most take under an hour.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Albuquerque, NM

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, so we replace failed fittings and damaged sections with parts matched to each material. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

Documentation is available.
