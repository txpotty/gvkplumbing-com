    Yard and Area Drain Cleaning in Sunland Park

    Yard drains and area inlets collect runoff from lawns, rock landscaping and patios. Over time the grates fill with leaves and the pipes fill with silt and small gravel. We pull the grates, vacuum the inlet box and jet the pipe toward its outlet. This restores the line so it can handle a full storm. We also check that grates are secure and seated.

    Root Removal from Storm Drains in Sunland Park

    Tree roots are a common cause of severe storm drain blockages in Sunland Park, especially in older neighborhoods with mature trees. Roots seek out moisture and can work into drain pipes through tiny cracks or loose joints, growing and eventually completely blocking water flow. GVK Plumbing uses specialized root-cutting attachments on our drain snakes and hydro jetting equipment to cut through and remove root intrusions. After removal, we can suggest further steps to prevent future root problems.

    Hydro Jetting for Storm Drains in Sunland Park, NM

    Hydro jetting is a proven solution for deeply clogged storm drains in Sunland Park, NM. This method uses high-pressure water streams to wash out stubborn obstructions like compacted soil, heavy silt, roots, and grease buildup. It is very effective for thoroughly cleaning the inside of the pipe walls, restoring the drain to its full capacity and preventing a quick return of blockages, a common issue with older drainage systems.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Sunland Park, NM
