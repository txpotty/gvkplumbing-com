    Disposal Installation in Sunland Park, NM: The Details

    Standard Disposal Installation in Sunland Park

    Most households in Sunland Park do well with a continuous-feed unit between 1/3 and 1/2 horsepower. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Then we wire it and test it. We listen for a smooth spin and watch the drain clear.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Sunland Park, NM

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Sunland Park

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. Power needs a check too. A larger motor pulls more current when it starts.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Sunland Park, NM

    Batch-feed units only run with the lid in place, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. They are slower to use. Parents of young kids often think that is worth it.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in Sunland Park, NM

    The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. The old disposal leaves with us.

    Garbage Disposal Installation Cost Factors in Sunland Park, NM

    The model and the power setup drive most of the cost. A plain swap with power already in place costs the least, and new wiring, a lowered drain or a reworked double bowl each add labor as a separate line. Unit and labor are quoted together.

    InSinkErator Model Selection in Sunland Park, NM

    We help you pick between the Badger and Evolution lines based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have. Badger units are compact and simple, while Evolution units are taller and heavier, with more grind stages and better sound insulation. Cabinet size often decides it. We measure before you buy.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Sunland Park, NM

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.
