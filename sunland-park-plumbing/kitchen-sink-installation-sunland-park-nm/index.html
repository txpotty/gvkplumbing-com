    Frequently Asked Questions About Kitchen Sink Installation in Sunland Park

    A basic drop-in kitchen sink install usually costs $300 to $600 for labor. Undermount and farmhouse installs usually run $450 to $1,200, and adding a disposal or relocating the drain adds $150 to $600.

    Top-mount (drop-in) sinks sit on top of the counter, with a visible rim, and are generally easier to install. Under-mount sinks are installed beneath the counter, creating a seamless look, but require more complex sealing and support for a secure installation.

    Most installs take three to five hours. Undermount sinks may need time for adhesive to set before the drain is fully loaded.

    We install Kohler, Blanco, Elkay, Kraus, Franke, Ruvati and many others. You can supply your sink or we can recommend one.

    Many standard cabinets need reinforcement for heavy fireclay or cast iron sinks. We add a support frame so the sink is safe.

    Signs that your kitchen sink needs replacement include persistent leaks, extensive rust or corrosion, deep scratches or cracks that cannot be repaired, or a kitchen remodel that calls for a new look. Frequent clogging might also point to an issue with the sink's drain assembly.

    Most standard kitchen sink installations can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. However, more complex jobs, such as installing a farmhouse sink, modifying cabinetry, or replacing significant plumbing lines, might take longer, potentially up to a full day.

    We install all major brands of kitchen sinks, including but not limited to Kohler, Elkay, Moen, Blanco, Franke, and InSinkErator for disposal-ready sinks. Our plumbers are experienced with various materials and mounting styles from these reputable manufacturers.
