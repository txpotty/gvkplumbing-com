    PEX Pipe Replacement in Sunland Park

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping has become a common choice for residential pipe replacement due to its flexibility, affordability, and resistance to freezing. Pipes in exterior walls, attics and unheated garages around Sunland Park are the most likely to freeze when a cold front moves through. Unlike rigid pipes, PEX can expand, reducing the risk of burst pipes during unexpected cold snaps. GVK Plumbing specializes in PEX installation, which often allows for less invasive replacement methods compared to traditional materials. This modern solution offers excellent performance and water quality for your home.
