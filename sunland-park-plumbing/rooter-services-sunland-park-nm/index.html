    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Sunland Park

    Yes, most rooter jobs come with a limited guarantee. The terms depend on the line and what we found, and they are written on your estimate. Roots are the exception. Broken pipe or heavy roots can shorten the guarantee because the roots keep coming back until the pipe is repaired.

    Plan on 1 to 3 hours for a single clogged drain. Heavy main line root cutting or a long jetting run can take 3 to 6 hours. You get a time estimate first. Once the tech sees the cleanout and asks which fixtures are slow, they can give you a closer estimate of how long it will run.

    Slow drains, gurgling pipes and sewer smell are the early ones. If the tub fills when the toilet flushes, the main is blocked. Do not wait on that one. More than one fixture draining slow at the same time usually means the main, not a branch.

    Chemical drain openers rarely clear a real clog and can eat at old pipe. Old cast iron and the thin brass traps found in Sunland Park's established homes take the most damage.     Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting is different. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe walls clean. Tough lines often get both, rooting to open the path and jetting to clean out what remains.

    Clay or older cast iron lines with trees nearby usually need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera look can set the timing. How quickly roots return after the last cut tells us if yearly or every-other-year service fits your line.
