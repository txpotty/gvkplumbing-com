    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Los Lunas

    Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale accumulates quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in hard water.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Los Lunas

Forgetting the knockout is one of the most common DIY mistakes and leads to a dishwasher that will not drain. We test both appliances together before we go.
