    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Los Lunas, NM

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. Sizing is the first thing we check. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    After-Hours Gas Line Repair in Los Lunas

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls. After you and your family are outside and the utility has made the scene safe, our plumber comes out to locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test. We coordinate with the utility on the meter.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Los Lunas

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Expansive clay soils in some areas swell and shrink with moisture, which can shift slab foundations and the pipes that run beneath them. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Los Lunas

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and add an accessible shutoff valve if the old one is missing or seized. Usually under an hour per appliance. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections, and most also need a sediment trap ahead of the appliance gas valve.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Los Lunas

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. Our plumbers total the BTU load, measure the run and size the pipe so the last appliance on the line still gets full pressure at peak use. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Los Lunas, NM

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Los Lunas, NM. Inspection covers sizing, approved materials, pipe supports, shutoffs, drip legs and a witnessed or documented pressure test. We handle the paperwork. A permitted repair also gives you a paper trail for insurance and for any future sale.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Los Lunas, NM

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Gas Leak Detection in Los Lunas

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. Our plumbers isolate the system section by section, sweep it with an electronic detector and confirm the exact spot with soap solution. After we locate the leak, we price the repair in writing.
