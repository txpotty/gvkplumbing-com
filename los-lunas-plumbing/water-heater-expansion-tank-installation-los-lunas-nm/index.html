    Many homes see high incoming pressure, so pressure reducing valves are widespread.     Tank Sizing and Placement in Los Lunas

    Correct sizing and placement are key to an effective water heater expansion tank in Los Lunas. An undersized tank will not provide adequate protection, while an oversized one is an avoidable expense. We accurately calculate the required tank volume based on your water heater's capacity, system pressure and average water temperature. Placement matters too, usually near the water heater on the cold water inlet line, with easy access for future maintenance or inspection.

    New Expansion Tank Installation in Los Lunas

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve and no expansion tank, the water heater is probably seeing a pressure spike every heating cycle. We install a new tank on the cold water supply near the heater using a tee and short nipple. The air side is precharged to match your static pressure, usually between 50 and 70 psi. We then run the heater and watch the gauge to confirm pressure stays steady.
