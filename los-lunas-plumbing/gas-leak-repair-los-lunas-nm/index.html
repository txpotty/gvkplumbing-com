    Gas Meter to House Line Repair in Los Lunas, NM

    The run from the utility meter to the house shutoff is the homeowner's pipe in most service areas, so the repair falls to a plumber and not the gas company. It is also the stretch that takes the most weather, sitting in the sun or the soil. Risers rust at grade. We replace the riser or the buried run, pressure test it and leave the meter connection ready for the utility to open the meter. Expect a half day for most riser swap.

    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Los Lunas, NM

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. We swap in a new coated stainless connector, add a quarter-turn ball valve and set a sediment trap where the code calls for it. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is lit again.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Los Lunas

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. More outdoor lines get hit by shovels and fence augers than fail from rust. Dead grass is a tell. We pressure test the outdoor branch on its own, locate the leak and replace the run with polyethylene and a tracer wire. Above-ground stub-ups get a fresh riser and a shutoff valve.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Los Lunas

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. In its place goes a coated stainless connector and a new shutoff valve, plus a drip leg if the code calls for it. Bubble testing follows before the pilot is lit.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Los Lunas

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it. We read the tag first, since it lists what the utility found. Then we test everything. After the repair, we run the pressure test the utility or inspector requires and send them the paperwork. Relights are scheduled with the utility, usually within a day.

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Los Lunas, NM

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. Repairs use the same brand's fittings as the existing tubing, since manufacturers do not allow mixing parts. A splice that skips that rule fails inspection.
