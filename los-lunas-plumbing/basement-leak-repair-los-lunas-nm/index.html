    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Los Lunas

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Los Lunas typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Exposed pipe on an unfinished ceiling is cheap. Opening finished walls or the slab adds cost, and a multi-floor cast iron stack can go past that range.

    Zoeller, Liberty and Wayne cover most of our installs because they are durable and parts are easy to get. We pick cast iron models for primary pumps. Battery or water-powered backups go in where power outages are common, plus a high-water alarm.

    Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. Expect two to four hours with drywall access. Seepage work ranges from half a day for one crack to several days for a perimeter drain.

    For sump pumps we mostly install Zoeller and Liberty, with Wayne for some battery backups. We prefer cast iron pump housings. For crack injection we use two-part polyurethane or epoxy kits from established commercial suppliers, and for interior drains we use perforated pipe with a dimpled wall membrane from the same manufacturer so the pieces fit together.

    Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Larger work is a different story. Replacing a drain stack, installing a new sump with a discharge line, adding a backwater valve, or excavating near the foundation may require a permit, and we pull it when it is needed.

    Look for damp spots on walls or floors, a musty smell, and white chalky deposits on concrete. That white crust is mineral left after water dries. Peeling paint low on the wall, rust on the water heater base, and a water bill that keeps creeping up with no change in use all point toward a leak worth testing. Smell often comes first.

    It depends on the scope. Small pipe repairs usually skip the permit. Replacing a drain stack, adding a sump discharge, installing a backwater valve or digging near the foundation may need one, and we check with the local building department and pull it before work starts so it does not hold up a sale later.

    Only some repairs need excavation. Outside membranes and new footing drains require digging to the footing. Pipe repairs, crack injection and interior drain systems are all done from inside the basement, which is why they are the more common choice on homes with patios, driveways or landscaping right against the foundation.
