    Sewer Camera Inspections in South Valley, NM

    Before any major sewer cleaning, especially for recurring issues, a sewer camera inspection is crucial. GVK Plumbing uses waterproof cameras to travel through your sewer lines, providing a live view of the pipe's interior. This lets us accurately locate the exact cause and position of a blockage, identify pipe damage like cracks or collapses, and choose the most effective cleaning method, saving time and avoiding unnecessary digging in South Valley, NM.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in South Valley, NM

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Homes built in the late 1970s through the mid 1990s may have polybutylene supply lines, which are known to fail and are worth replacing. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.

    Dealing with Grease Buildup in South Valley, NM

    Grease accumulation is a frequent cause of sewer line clogs, particularly from kitchen drains. Over time, grease solidifies and narrows the pipe, leading to slow drains and backups. Our cleaning methods, especially hydro jetting, are very effective at breaking down and flushing away grease buildup, restoring proper flow to your pipes in South Valley.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in South Valley

    Some homes have no accessible cleanout, which forces techs to pull a toilet or go through a roof vent.     Root Removal From Sewer Lines in South Valley

    Trees and shrubs send roots toward any moisture they can find, and a sewer joint is a steady source during dry months. Thirsty yard trees and shrubs are common culprits in local yards. We use a sectional cable with a root saw sized to the pipe to cut the mass free and pull it out. Heavy root growth may need a follow-up with a jetter to clear fine root hairs. A camera inspection then shows where roots are entering so you can decide on lining or repair.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Cleaning in South Valley

    If sewage is coming up through a shower or floor drain, stop using water and call right away. We send a tech with a main line machine, protective gear and a wet vac.     Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in South Valley, NM

    Cleaning removes what is in the pipe, but it cannot fix a crack, a belly or a collapsed section. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera will usually show why. We compare the yearly cost of maintenance cleanings to a spot repair, lining or replacement. Sometimes regular cleaning is the sensible move for a few years. Other times a repair now saves more over time, and we show you the math.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in South Valley, NM

    Grease from kitchens cools and coats the inside of sewer pipes, and minerals in the water bond with it to form a tough crust. Water across New Mexico is generally hard and mineral-heavy, so scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucets and appliance valves. Over years the pipe opening shrinks until flow slows to a trickle. A cable can punch a hole through, but jetting cleans the walls back to full size. We choose the method based on pipe material and condition. Cleaning before the line fully closes spares you money and stress.
