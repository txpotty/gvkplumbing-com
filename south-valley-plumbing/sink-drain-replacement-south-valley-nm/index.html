    Push-Button Drain Installation in South Valley, NM

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in South Valley

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. They all get set together. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in South Valley, NM

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked arm or rusted stub drips down the back panel, out of sight, until the wood swells or smells. We cut back to solid pipe. Then new PVC gets tied in.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in South Valley

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. Old strainer putty dries out and the locknut rusts, so water runs down the tailpiece and looks like a trap leak. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.
